# Drone Siege without the player (spec 132, 1871): a small autonomous turret platform holds the post alone. The player's ship leaves;
# the platform keeps the area live and the clock running, the drone waves come, and the post lasts the whole hold.
# Claim Jumpers raiders go for the barge (spec 137), so a one-gun platform is not enough there: mechanics-guard-hold.
# missions: random
# timeout: 900
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
mech_generate siege
mech_accept siege
mech_hold_goto
pause off
wait 3
expect mech_hold_guarded == 1
# Computer manning a small laser, a targeted reactor, no thrusters: the cheapest gun platform (spec 1050 to 1053), next to the post.
auto_build Guard -400,0 yarden_zamir.computer@-1,1,1 cosmoteer.laser_blaster_small@0,0 yarden_zamir.targeted_reactor@-1,3
wait 1
waitfor auto_manned == 1 10
# The player gives it rows, like any autonomous ship (spec 1061): the Defensive preset fights what comes near.
tactics_ship Guard
tactics Defensive
tacticsauto on
tostation
pause off
wait 10
expect mech_hold_guarded == 1
expect mech_hold_seconds >= 10
waitfor mech_wave_ships >= 1 90
expect mech_waves_vs_player >= 1
# The platform engages the drones (its target row matches) and draws their fire; it stands next to the post, because its laser reaches 260.
waitfor tactics_target_row >= 1 150
waitfor mech_goal_siege == 1 450
expect mech_state_siege == 0
quit
