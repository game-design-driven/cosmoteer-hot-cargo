# Issue #289 (spec 2220, 2221): an enemy offered its surrender right after it attacked, and its page offered the players' own
# surrender to it. Now it offers its surrender only after SurrenderMinFightSeconds in the fight or SurrenderMinDamage lost, and its
# page then shows only the vanilla accept and decline. Vanilla decides alone here (RiskEnabled false), so the stance does not matter.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
aiset FleeEnabled false
scav_set SpawnChance 0
fr_set PayForwardChance 0
openspace 6000
fr_kill_enemies
dip_set RiskEnabled false
dip_set DemandHailChance 0
dip_set SurrenderMinFightSeconds 12
dip_set SurrenderMinDamage 0.95
# A small pirate ship next to the player ship with a tenth of its health gone: vanilla lets it offer its surrender at once (any
# damage is enough). Its value ratio roll is set, so the ship ID does not decide. The player ship's guns stay on: vanilla needs the
# players' firepower.
aispawn pirates 1 1
# Right after the spawn, before the ship's first AI tick, which makes the vanilla rolls (#336: that tick undid a roll set before it).
dip_ai_offers
ainew_watch 0 300
aidamage new 0.9
wait 3
# A few seconds into the fight it does not offer its surrender, and its page offers the players' surrender.
expect dip_offers == 0
dip_page aispawn
expect dip_page_offersurrender == 1
# The fault: without the fight time, vanilla offers it now.
dip_set SurrenderMinFightSeconds 0
expect dip_offers == 1
dip_set SurrenderMinFightSeconds 12
# After the fight time it offers its surrender, and its page no longer offers the players' surrender or a demand.
wait 12
expect dip_offers == 1
dip_page aispawn
expect dip_page_offersurrender == 0
expect dip_page_demand == 0
# Enough damage is enough on its own: with a long fight time, the ship at 30 % of its health offers once the damage limit is 50 %.
dip_set SurrenderMinFightSeconds 1000
aidamage new 0.3
expect dip_offers == 0
dip_set SurrenderMinDamage 0.5
expect dip_offers == 1
quit
