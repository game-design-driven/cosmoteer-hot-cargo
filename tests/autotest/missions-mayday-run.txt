# Mayday victim (spec 2431): it runs to the players with its attackers on its radar. With the vanilla Normal AI it started fast, then
# turned its hull to an attacker (forced rotation, as the escort freighter in #372) and crawled at 5 to 7 units per second once the
# attackers reached it. Its own move order keeps it at cruise speed (about 30 for a Gannet).
newgame
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
hc_mayday_start
pause off
hc_track hc_mayday_victim
# The attackers reach it in about 10 s.
wait 10
hc_track_mark
wait 10
expect hc_track_closed > 150
expect hc_track_turned == 0
quit
