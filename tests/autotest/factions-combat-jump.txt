# Factions: a jump in a fight (spec 653).
# The player ship gets a small hyperdrive and spins up at a relay like a real jump. Then hunters arrive, a save block (like a multi-step explosion) is held, and ENGAGE is pressed: vanilla would drop
# the jump; here it waits for the block and then starts.
newgame
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
pause off
fa_drive
wait 1
fa_torelay
wait 2
fa_gatejump
waitfor fa_preparing == 1 60
waitfor fa_ready == 1 120
heat 80
waitfor fa_combat >= 1 30
fa_saveblock 15
wait 1
expect fa_saveblocked == 1
fa_engage
wait 5
expect fa_jumpstarts == 0
waitfor fa_jumpstarts == 1 60
log jump started after the save block
quit
