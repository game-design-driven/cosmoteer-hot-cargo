# Number breakdowns on hover (spec 2580 to 2586): the heat rate and tech value lines of the heat panel, the trade tab price line, and the
# surrender, bluff and pay-off buttons of a hunter's demand page. Each check opens the tooltip of the real widget through the game's
# tooltip box, reads the real tooltip label, and needs its total line to show the number that the sim adds, the roll uses or the price
# patch applies.
