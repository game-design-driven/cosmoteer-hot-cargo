# Surrender odds (spec 1525, 1530): a badly damaged enemy does not demand the surrender of a stronger player fleet.
# Playtest bug: a half-destroyed ship hailed six player ships and asked them to surrender for a big fee.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
playerweapons off
scav_set SpawnChance 0
fr_set PayForwardChance 0
openspace 6000
fr_kill_enemies
# One big Monolith ship, at a quarter of its health. The player ship is 300 from it, five more player ships are in the fight 1,800 to
# 3,000 away (center to center; tier 1-2 hulls are under 300 in radius). Vanilla counted only ships within 1,500 of the enemy, ignored
# part damage and compared one player ship's cost, so it demanded. The five ships are placed from the enemy, not from the player ship:
# random hulls next to the player ship can reach into the 1,500 circle, and then vanilla does not demand (CI full suites, #184).
# A fixed hull: the vanilla rule weighs firepower and cost, so a random tier-6 pick demanded or not by chance (#184).
aispawn monolith 1 6 hull=Howitzer
ainew_watch 0 300
aidamage new 0.25
aiplayership 1800
aiplayership 1800
aiplayership 1800
aiplayership 1800
aiplayership 1800
wait 3
expect ai_humanships >= 6
# The fault: the vanilla rule alone demands (it ignores part damage, the player ships beyond 1,500 and all but one player ship's cost).
dip_set RiskEnabled false
expect dip_demands == 1
# The risk model: the damaged ship against the players' whole fleet does not demand.
dip_set RiskEnabled true
expect dip_stance < 3
expect dip_demands == 0
# Friends far away do not count (spec 1521): two more Monolith ships 3,500 away add no ally; one moved to 500 counts.
# Other ships of the system can be near, so the check is the change.
dip_mark_allies
aispawn monolith 2 3
ainear 1 0 3500
ainear 2 0 3500
wait 1
expect dip_risk_allies_gain == 0
ainear 1 0 500
wait 1
expect dip_risk_allies_gain == 1
quit
