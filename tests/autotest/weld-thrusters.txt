# Weld (spec 2483): a cut-off piece of the starter ship (thrusters, a laser, crew quarters) is welded back at a half turn. A thruster's
# part rect is larger than its physical tile, and the design keeps one part per tile of the rect: a place where such a rect covers a
# ship tile is not valid. Without that rule, the merge failed in the game's door search ("Internal Error") or with two design parts
# on one tile.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set StationEventChance 0
ev_set ArrivalEventChance 0
ev_set CourierChance 0
godmode on
pause off
wait 3
cut_mark
cut_clean
waitfor cut_splits == 1 240
weld_mark cut
weld_target piece
weld_note
wait 1
weld_auto 2
waitfor weld_orders == 1 10
waitfor weld_merges == 1 240
expect weld_parts_kept == 1
quit
