# Factions: relay jumps (spec 650). Every jump cuts heat: a relay with hunters near it by RelayWatchedHeatCut (0.75),
# a relay with no hunter near it by RelayClearHeatCut (0.85).
# Headless slots draw no frames, so the sim stays paused after a jump (see journey-jump.txt): one jump per game.
# Time heat after the arrival adds up to about 1 in the waits.

# Clear relay. The hunters spawn near the fleet at the start, far from the relays.
newgame
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
fa_tooltip
heat 80
wait 2
expect fa_huntersnear == 0
fa_jump relay
# The jump runs in real time (no run tick, spec 1007): wait for the arrival, not a fixed time.
waitfor sector == 2 20
wait 2
expect heat >= 11.5
expect heat <= 13.5

# Watched relay: the fleet waits at the relay, so the hunters spawn near it. 80 x 0.25 = 20.
newgame
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
fa_torelay
wait 2
heat 80
wait 2
expect fa_huntersnear >= 1
fa_tooltip
fa_jump relay
# The jump runs in real time (no run tick, spec 1007): wait for the arrival, not a fixed time.
waitfor sector == 2 20
wait 2
expect heat >= 19.5
expect heat <= 21.5
quit
