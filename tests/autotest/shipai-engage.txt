# Engage range (spec 401, 402, 406): an Aggressive ship does not chase an enemy far away on the team radar, and a Defensive ship
# does not leave to help an ally that is far away. A near enemy still pulls the ship, and a large AllyHelpRadius gives the old reach.
newgame
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
shieldplayers on
shipai Log on
aiset FleeOnVanilla false
aiset CallOnVanilla false
aiset CounterKiteOnVanilla false
tactics_ship Model-L
# A pirate without thrusters stays where the test puts it. 1700 from the player: on the radar (1807), beyond the engage range (1500).
aispawn pirates 1 1
aiheavy
aiaway 1700
tactics Aggressive
tacticsauto on
wait 8
expect tactics_target_row == -1
# Within the engage range the ship engages.
aiaway 1200
waitfor tactics_target_row >= 0 15
# Defensive: the pirate attacks a second player ship 3000 away (west, clear of the sun). The ally help radius (2000) keeps this
# ship with the fleet.
tacticsauto off
aiaway -3000
aiplayership 150
tactics Defensive
tacticsauto on
wait 8
expect tactics_target_row == -1
# A large radius gives the old reach: the ship goes to help.
shipai AllyHelpRadius 20000
waitfor tactics_target_row == 1 15
quit
