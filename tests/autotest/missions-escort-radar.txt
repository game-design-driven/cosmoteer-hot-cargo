# Escort freighter and an enemy on its radar (#372, spec 36): a hostile platform 1,000 beside the route, out of the freighter's sight
# circle and gun reach. The freighter keeps its course and speed. Before the fix, the vanilla aggro module turned its hull to the
# platform (forced rotation), and it crawled at 2 to 40 units per second.
# missions: random
newgame run
wait 8

hc_generate escort
hc_accept escort
hc_escort_goto
pause off
wait 3
hc_escort_ready

pause off

wait 3
hc_escort_diag watcher
expect hc_escort_turned == 0
hc_mark
waitfor hc_escort_progress > 200 20
expect hc_escort_turned == 0
hc_escort_diag after
