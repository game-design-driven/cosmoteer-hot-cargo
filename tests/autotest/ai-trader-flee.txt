# Trader flee hysteresis (spec 1531): a fleeing trade ship keeps fleeing while an enemy is within radar × TraderFleeStopFactor.
# Each step waits 2 s (the trade ship AI checks every 0.75 to 1.25 s) and the whole flee stays short: vanilla jumps a fleeing
# trade ship away after 10 to 20 s.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
godmode on
playerweapons off
openspace 5000
# Only the player is an enemy of the trade ship: other hostile ships near it also hold its flee.
fr_kill_enemies all
dip_spawn trader 600
# A new ship sets its radar radius at its first update.
wait 1
ai_trader_want
ai_trader_place 0.5
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 1
# Just outside the radar radius: vanilla stopped here and turned back; now the ship keeps fleeing.
ai_trader_place 1.25
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 1
ai_trader_place 1.25
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 1
# Outside the stop radius: the ship stops fleeing.
ai_trader_place 2
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 0
# Factor 1 is vanilla: a new trade ship stops at the radar radius.
aiset TraderFleeStopFactor 1
dip_spawn trader 600
wait 1
ai_trader_want
ai_trader_place 0.5
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 1
ai_trader_place 1.25
wait 2
expect ai_trader_fleeing == 0
quit
