# Co-op "Share resources" switch screenshots (spec 1318 to 1320): the switch off, then on with its chat line.
# Host in a windowed test slot (graphics), client in a headless test slot (scripts/screens-mp.sh). {PORT} is the host port.
render on
mphost {PORT}
mpstart 2
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp pu_set Enabled false
closepanel
waitfor mpmark >= 1 120
wait 2
screenshot share-off
share on
waitfor share_on == 1 10
waitfor share_hud == 1 5
wait 1
screenshot share-on
mp mpmark 2
waitfor mpconnected == 0 120
quit
