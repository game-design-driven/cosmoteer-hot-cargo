# Co-op session: enemy scaling, diplomacy, missions, AI personalities, currents, the loss timer and the win.
# See coop-core.txt for "client:", "as 1", "on 1" and steps.
# Before the join: the host's enemy slider is 2.0 and the client's is 1.0. The host's value counts, so every step of this
# session runs with an enemy factor of 2 (2 players) or 3 (3 players).
enemysetting 2.0
client: enemysetting 1.0
mphost {PORT}
mpstart {PLAYERS}
mp godmode on
mp ev_set Enabled false

step scaling.generation
# Spec 330 to 336: the host's value reaches the client. Both machines generate the first system with factor 2:
# pirate wanderers are round([14, 16] x Area) = 4 at SystemScale 0.5 (mod/contracts/actions.rules), so 8. The state hash compares the ship counts.
# With 3 players the factor is 1 + (2 - 1) x 2 = 3 (spec 331), so 12. The group spacing shrinks with the factor, so the
# system has room for all of them (spec 332).
expect enemysetting == 2
@2p expect enemyfactor == 2
@3p expect enemyfactor == 3
on 1 expect enemysetting == 2
@2p on 1 expect enemyfactor == 2
@3p on 1 expect enemyfactor == 3
counttag pirate
@2p expect tagcount == 8
@3p expect tagcount == 12

step diplomacy.payoff
# Spec 587 (#25): the client ticks tech on the hunter page, and the draft is shared, so the host adds money to it. The client
# sends the pay-off through the shared choice input. Both machines see the hunters leave and the same vault tech.
mp aiset FleeEnabled false
mp aiset CallEnabled false
mp dip_set DemandHailChance 0
mp dip_set MercChance 0
mp dip_set HunterLeaveSeconds 2
mp dip_set HireChanceBase 5
mp dip_set PayChanceCap 1
mp dip_set PayMoneyChance 5
# Factor 2 sends 2 hunters, and each other hunter near the hailed one lowers the chance (the crowd factor). The step needs a sure pay-off.
mp dip_set PayMaxOtherHunters 1000
as 1 spawnstage Warning
waitfor hunters >= 1 30
as 1 dip_near hunters
# The fleet's guns can kill the picked hunter before the pay-off (a queue run failed so); the shield keeps it alive.
mp dip_shield hunter
wait 2
as 1 dip_pick hunter tech 2
waitfor dip_picks >= 1 30
dip_pick hunter money 5
waitfor dip_picks >= 2 30
as 1 dip_payoff hunter
waitfor dip_vaulttech == 6 30
waitfor dip_leaving >= 1 60
waitfor hunters == 0 60
mp dip_shield off

step sharedchoice.listen-hunter
# Spec 1131 to 1137 in vote mode: the host talks to a hunter and the client listens. Both vote to fight; the vote is unanimous on
# both machines. The host's dialogue with an enemy pauses the game (vanilla setting), so the vote decides in its input (spec 604).
# The client's listen window closes after the host's channel.
# A hunter to talk to: a Warning wave on both machines.
mp spawnstage Warning
waitfor hunters >= 1 30
mp sc_reset
votemode on
waitfor sc_votemode == 1 10
mp dip_near hunter0
# Sight and comms range need a moment after the move: wait for the vanilla call rules, not for a fixed time.
listen_target hunter0
waitfor listen_reach == 1 30
dip_open hunter0
on 1 waitfor listen_hails == 1 30
on 1 listen 1
on 1 listen_click Come and take it
# With 3 players the second client listens too, so the vote can be unanimous.
@3p on 2 waitfor listen_hails == 1 30
@3p on 2 listen 1
@3p on 2 listen_click Come and take it
waitfor comms_vote_marks == 1 30
on 1 waitfor listen_vote_marks == 1 30
comms_click Come and take it
waitfor sc_decided_reason == 1 30
on 1 waitfor sc_decided_reason == 1 30
votemode off
waitfor sc_votemode == 0 10
dip_close
on 1 waitfor listen_open == 0 30
pause off
waitfor ev_paused == 0 10

step missions.trap
# A forced trap. The client kills the ambush; the reward pays both players. Spec 331 raises the base 2 ambushers.
mp hc_generate shady
mp hc_accept shady
waitfor hc_trap_state == 0 20
mp hc_shady_spring
waitfor hc_trap_state == 2 20
expect hc_ambushers >= 2
hc_mark
on 1 hc_mark
as 1 hc_kill_ambush
waitfor hc_trap_done == 1 30
expect hc_money_gain > 0
on 1 expect hc_money_gain > 0

step mechanics.mining-escort
# Spec 1850, 1860 to 1866: the client answers the miner's "Ready" from its machine (the shared choice input). The miner mines
# and takes in dropped ore on both machines, the two waves come at the same ore count, and it docks. The state hash compares the objective.
mp mech_generate miningescort
mp mech_accept miningescort
mp mech_mine_goto
pause off
waitfor mech_mine_hailed == 1 60
on 1 mech_mine_ready
waitfor mech_mine_phase == 1 15
on 1 waitfor mech_mine_phase == 1 15
mp mech_mine_field
pause off
waitfor mech_mine_ore > 0 120
mp mech_mine_drop 40
waitfor mech_mine_phase == 3 240
on 1 waitfor mech_mine_phase == 3 60
expect mech_mine_waves == 2
on 1 expect mech_mine_waves == 2
mp mech_mine_dock
pause off
waitfor mech_goal_miningescort == 1 60
on 1 waitfor mech_goal_miningescort == 1 60
mp debt_kill_enemies

step ai.personalities
# Spec 369: hostile pirates and hunters with personalities, cover rocks and teams fight both players' ships.
# Spec 1303: the starting control room stands in for the sensor array, so calls in this fight are intercepted on both machines.
# The player ships take no damage and lose no crew: the step tests the AI, not who wins (a Mac run 2026-11-18 lost the client's ship).
mp shieldplayers on
mp tacticlog on
mp aiset CallInterceptParts cosmoteer.control_room_small
mp airocks 6 600
mp aispawn pirates 3 2
mp spawnstage Strike
# Spec 196: a call starts when the players see the caller. The Strike hunters come next to the host's ship, in its sight.
wait 1
mp hunternear all 600
mp aispawn monolith 3 2
# Spec 1313: the spawned pirates and Monolith ships fall back and pick repair plans on both machines.
mp aidamage new 0.3
wait 40
aiprofiles host
expect ai_personality > 0
on 1 aiprofiles client
on 1 expect ai_personality > 0
expect ai_call_intercept >= 1
on 1 expect ai_call_intercept >= 1
expect tactic_fallback >= 1
on 1 expect tactic_fallback >= 1
mp shieldplayers off

step pursuit.coop
# Spec 912, 934, in a new run: the client's stage start sends a reporting scout, and its report raises heat on both machines.
# Then the client pays the hacker through the shared choice input: only the client pays, and both machines start the same
# outage. The paid outage is not a natural one in the pacer.
mprestart
# Autotests start with the Pursuit off (spec 943); this new run turns it on.
mp pu_set Enabled true
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp aiset FleeEnabled false
mp aiset CallEnabled false
mp aiset RegroupEnabled false
mp dip_set DemandHailChance 0
mp pu_set ScoutChance 1
mp pu_set BlockadeChance 0
as 1 stage
waitfor pu_scouts == 1 30
mp pu_scoutnear 300
waitfor pu_scouts == 0 60
# Warning starts at 40, the report adds 15.
expect heat >= 54.5
on 1 expect heat >= 54.5
mp pu_hacker
waitfor pu_hacker == 1 30
mp pu_setmoney 50000
mp pu_hacker_near 400
wait 3
pu_money
on 1 pu_money
on 1 pu_hacker_pick pay
waitfor pu_outage >= 200 30
expect pu_count_outage == 0
expect pu_hacker_done == 1
expect pu_moneyspent == 0
on 1 expect pu_moneyspent == 30000
on 1 expect pu_outage >= 200

step hunters.anchor
# Spec 1537 to 1539, in a new run: the client flies 16,000 away from the host. A hunter wave spawns near one player ship, not at the
# center between the fleets (far from both, where vanilla puts ships into stasis), so the hunters stay live.
mprestart
mp godmode on
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp aiset FleeEnabled false
mp aiset CallEnabled false
mp dip_set DemandHailChance 0
as 1 apart 16000
wait 3
mp spawnstage Warning
waitfor hunters >= 1 30
# The stasis manager takes ships out of the live area within a few seconds.
wait 8
expect hunters >= 1
expect hunterstasis == 0
on 1 expect hunterstasis == 0

step thieves.collect
# Spec 1783, in a new run: a thief group fights the host's ship, the spilled cargo stops it, it collects and leaves. The phases and
# the counts are run state, and the state hash compares them, so a divergence fails the step. Every setup line runs on both machines.
mprestart
mp godmode on
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp scav_set FactionEnabled false
mp th_set Enabled true
mp th_set JoinChance 0
mp th_cargo me steel 200
mp th_encounter
mp th_near 600
waitfor th_attacking >= 1 60
mp th_spill steel 400
waitfor th_phase == 1 20
on 1 waitfor th_phase == 1 20
# The players' turrets may kill some while they collect with their guns off; the others flee and jump out.
waitfor th_count == 0 240
on 1 waitfor th_count == 0 60

step journey.buyer-win
# A new run: the Buyer appears, the client flies to it and sells tech from its own machine, and the run is won on both.
mprestart
# The loss steps before turned godmode off. Without it, ships near the Buyer can destroy the client's vaults before the sale.
mp godmode on
# The player ships take no damage and lose no crew: the step tests the sale and the win, not a fight near the Buyer.
# Full suite 44186766891: the client's ship became a wreck after the win.
mp shieldplayers on
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp buyer spawn
waitfor buyers == 1 30
# The first goto loads the Buyer out of stasis, the second puts the client's ship next to it.
as 1 buyer goto
wait 15
as 1 buyer goto
wait 3
on 1 expect buyertrades == 1
on 1 buyer sell 2
waitfor won == 1 90
expect delivered >= 1
expect score > 0
# Heat stays off after the win.
mp heat -100
wait 10
expect heat == 0

end
