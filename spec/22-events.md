## 22. Events

FTL-style choice events. EVENTS.md holds the design and the catalog.

67. An event is a page with a title, a text, 1 to 4 choices and up to 4 options (requirement 810). Event content is in `mod/events/events.rules`. Chain pages are events in `mod/chains/pages.rules` (requirement 167). Ships that outcomes spawn are generators in `mod/events/spawns.rules`; they spawn around a ship of the player who chose (requirement 1537). A change to these files needs a game restart.
68. A choice has an optional sure cost, an optional need and 1 or more weighted outcomes. A submit choice also pays the selected options (requirement 811). The window shows the cost on the button. A choice whose need or cost the fleet cannot meet is greyed out with the reason in red, or hidden when the rules say so. The need of a choice includes its cost.
69. Triggers (the Courier Job offer in requirement 85 is not an event and has its own roll; the Wanted trigger is in requirement 471; the Relic and Hail triggers are in requirements 765 and 769; abandoned ships hold loot without an event, requirement 764; a Station event shows in the station's comms, requirement 768): Arrival (`ArrivalDelaySeconds` after the first arrival in a system, chance `ArrivalEventChance`), Signal (a player ship within `SignalTriggerDistance` of a signal POI; the POI goes away), Station (the first trade station within `StationTriggerDistance` in a system visit, chance `StationEventChance`; never the Buyer). No event opens at the start of a run. The run's first system has no jump in, so its Arrival roll comes when the start cooldown (requirement 70) ends. There, the Arrival and Station chances are × `FirstSystemChanceFactor` (1.5), at most 1, and the Station roll waits for that cooldown instead of being used up inside it. With the defaults, system 1 has an event in about 97 % of visits that reach a station (1 − 0.1 × 0.25), and in 90 % of visits that do not.
70. Arrival and Station events wait `CooldownSeconds` after the last triggered event, and after the start of a run. A system gets at most `MaxEventsPerSystem` Arrival and Station events. An event opens at most `MaxPerRun` times per run (rules, default 1; 0 means no limit). Only one event page waits at a time.
71. A trigger picks one eligible event by weight: `Weight + WeightPerHeat × heat + WeightPerTech × fleet tech count`, never below 0. Outcomes use the same weight rule. The outcome roll uses a seed from the simulation random source, taken when the page opens.
72. Outcome effects: money, money in tech value, fame (gain or loss), heat, reputation (requirement 81), the Buyer price (requirement 84), clearing wanted (requirement 472), tech units (through the Tech Vaults), crew, vanilla resources, hull damage, spawned ships, story flags and a follow-up page. Money gains are split between the human players like a mission reward. The chooser pays money costs.
73. Event money unit: 1 = the middle of a standard mission's pay at the sector tier × the economy money reward factor × `RewardScale` × `MoneyScale`. A fame unit is a standard mission's fame × the economy fame reward factor × `RewardScale` × `FameScale`. Heat effects × `HeatScale`. Money amounts use the tier and the tech value from when the page opened.
74. A choice goes through a multiplayer input (`HotCargoSharedChoice`, requirement 603). The input carries the page serial, so a second click on the same page (by another player) does nothing. The input checks the chooser's need again before it applies the choice. If the sim rejects a choice, the chooser gets a chat line with the reason, and the buttons work again after 3 seconds. A trigger checks money needs against the human player with the most money, because any player can choose.
75. After a choice, the window shows the outcome text and one result line per effect. A follow-up page shows the outcome text above its own text.
76. The event state is saved in the run state: seen counts, flags and the sector at which each flag was set, the waiting page, the last result, the cooldown, the per-system counts and the systems that already rolled a Courier Job.
77. In single player, an open event window pauses the game when `PauseOnEvent` is on. Autotest games do not pause, also with a window, unless the test asks for it (`ev_pause on`). The window fits its height to the page, and it opens only after the run intro closes.
78. A content error in an event (a missing spawn, an unknown resource, a bad reference) shows in the overlay at the first event and turns off events for that game. An error while an outcome applies drops the page and shows the error; the game keeps running.
79. Event-spawned ships that fight the players are never tagged `hc_hunter`, so the Hunters area does not count them toward `MaxLiveHunters` and they keep their own hostile player index. Local bounty hunters (Wanted Broadcast) use local faction ship designs, the tag `hc_ev_bounty` and a bounty mission.
80. A need can require a flag that was set at least N sectors ago (`FlagAges`). The event state saves the sector index at which each flag was last set.
81. An outcome can change reputation with the faction that controls the system, in units of a standard mission's reputation at the sector tier. A system without a controlling faction gets no change. A fame loss lowers fame and never goes below 0.
82. A choice cost can grow with heat: `Money + MoneyPerHeat × heat` money units, with the heat from when the page opened.
83. A need can bound the sectors left before the Buyer's sector (`RunSectorCount` − sector index): `MinSectorsLeft` and `MaxSectorsLeft` (−1 = no limit).
84. An outcome can multiply the run's Buyer price multiplier (`BuyerPrice`, above 0 and at most 1). The run state saves the multiplier. The Buyer sale price and the score use it (requirement 7).
85. Courier Job: the first trade station that a player ship comes near in a system rolls `CourierChance` once for the whole run. On success, a Courier Job offer appears in that station's mission list. The job: the accept loads a sealed case into the hold (requirement 1101); within 7 minutes, hand it to a neutral contact 8,000 to 12,000 times `SystemScale` (requirement 105) away in the contact's comms (requirements 1102 to 1104), then collect the pay at the station. Pay: 1.25 × a standard mission's money, 1 × its fame.
86. Event missions carry contract terms (economy, requirement 100 and later) as a `HotCargoContract` reward. Missions that events add straight to the log have zero terms: no fee, heat, trail or informant. The Courier Job: free to accept, flat heat 3, trail weight 0.5, informant chance 15 %.
87. An event can be in the Unavoidable category: every choice costs something, with no way to avoid it. A trigger picks at most `MaxUnavoidablePerRun` (default 1) Unavoidable events per run, counted from the seen counts. Vault Coolant Leak is the only one so far.
88. Dead in Space: a broken-down freighter needs repair supplies or a tow. It opens as an Arrival event or as a follow-up of the Distress Beacon. The tow choice needs a Tractor Beam Emitter and starts a Tow Job mission: the freighter has no AI, and the mission completes when it is within 1,200 of the nearest trade station at least 3,000 from it (`HotCargoTow` objective). It fails when the freighter is destroyed. The Rescue Beacon reuses this objective with the players' own ship as the towed ship (requirement 1147).
89. Event texts show no counts that live elsewhere (starting tech, spawn counts). The token `{crates}` shows the live fleet tech count. The choice buttons have no number prefix; the UX area draws the key numbers.

### Station under attack

Code: `src/HotCargo/Events/StationAttack.cs`. Numbers: the `Events.StationAttack*` and `Events.StationRebuild*` tunables.

90. **Trigger.** On the first visit of a system, from sector `StationAttackMinSector` (2), the attack roll waits `StationAttackDelaySeconds` (40 s) after the arrival. Then it runs once, when a player ship's hull first comes within `StationAttackTriggerDistance` (1,500) of the hull of a live trade station. The Buyer's station and a station that refuses calls (requirement 657) do not count. The roll needs events on, no attack that runs, and the Pursuit pacer (kind `StationAttack`, requirements 905 and 906). It uses `sim.Rand` with the chance `StationAttackChance` (0.2).
91. **Raiders.** On a success, the `StationRaiders` spawn (`mod/events/spawns.rules`) puts 3 combat ships of the local factions, 2 tiers above the system, 300 outside the station's hull, on the far side from the nearest player ship. They have one unique allegiance (enemies of the station and of the players) and the hunter AI, without the `hc_hunter` tag (requirement 79). Their first strike destroys the share `StationAttackGunsDown` (0.9) of the weapon parts of the station and of its defense platforms (vanilla tag `station_defense`, within 1,000 of its hull), nearest to the raiders first. A raider that spawns out of the live range waits in stasis and stays in the attack. With no live raider there is no attack, and the stasis raiders are removed.
92. **Warning.** When the attack starts, a center text and a chat line say that raiders attack the station. The station hails with the `station_attack` page ("Mayday"). With another page open, the chat line alone carries the warning (requirement 70). The page says that a shot that hits the station counts against the players (the vanilla wanted rule). The station's blip shows UNDER ATTACK and each raider's blip shows RAIDER. During the rebuild the station shows REBUILDING.
93. **Targets.** A raider attacks the station. A hunter override (`HunterOrders.Overrides`, above every hunter tactic except fall back, call and flee) gives the order. A raider turns on a player ship only while that ship is in its way: its hull is within `StationAttackInTheWayRange` (150) of the raider's hull, or it is on the line of fire between the raider and the station. The nearest such ship is the target. Limit: as in vanilla, turrets that cannot bear on the target fire on other enemies in range, so a player ship near a raider can take some fire.
94. **End.** The attack ends when no raider is left (destroyed or a wreck): the station repels it. It ends when the station's health (`HunterOrders.HealthFraction`) is below `StationAttackFallHealth` (0.8), or the station is gone: the station falls. It ends after `StationAttackMaxSeconds` (240) of attack time: the station holds. While the station is in stasis (the players are far away), the attack and its time wait. When the station falls or holds, the raiders that are left jump out after 5 s (the Diplomacy leave list), and the raiders in stasis are removed.
95. **Help and reward.** The players help when a human player's shot damages a raider during the attack. When the station repels the attack with help, the players get `StationAttackReward` (1) event money units, split like a mission reward, and `StationAttackReputation` (1) reputation units with the station's faction. The station thanks them. With no help, or when the station falls or holds, there is no reward.
96. **Rebuild.** After the attack, a station with destroyed parts rebuilds them from the debris near it. From the start of the attack to the end of the rebuild, the vanilla self-repair of the station rebuilds no destroyed part; it still repairs damaged parts. Each `StationRebuildStepSeconds` (1 s), the station takes the unclaimed debris piece nearest to its hull within `StationRebuildRadius` (1,000). Unclaimed: junk that nobody holds or needs (requirement 1241 without the size limit). A wreck that holds resources is loot, and the station leaves it alone. The station pulls the piece toward its hull at `StationRebuildPullSpeed` (40). Within `StationRebuildReach` (20) of the hull, it takes in `StationRebuildPartsPerStep` (3) parts of the piece, nearest first. For each part it rebuilds `StationRebuildPerDebrisPart` (2) destroyed parts at no cost, with the vanilla rule: a part must touch the hull, and nothing may block it. An empty piece leaves the sim. A chat line marks the start and the end of the rebuild.
97. **Fallback.** The rebuild ends with no debris in reach for `StationRebuildWaitSeconds` (60), or when no destroyed part can go back. Then the vanilla self-repair rebuilds the rest from the station's stock. In stasis the rebuild waits. A jump of the players to another system ends an attack or a rebuild. Limit: raiders that stay behind fight on with the hunter AI.
98. **Co-op, save and tests.** The attack runs in the sim tick, the AI tick and the damage call. It reads and writes the run state (`EventsState.Attack`, `AttackDueSeconds`), and its rolls use `sim.Rand`. Text and tags are UI only. A save and a load keep an attack or a rebuild. Tests: `events-station-attack` (the sector limit, the warning, the raiders on the station, a player ship in the way, the pay for help, the rebuild from debris across a save and load, a fall). Unit tests: `StationAttackModelTests`.

### The troublemaker

Code: `src/HotCargo/Friendly/Troublemaker.cs`. Page: `troublemaker` in `mod/events/events.rules`. Numbers: the `Friendly.Troublemaker*` tunables.

480. **Offer.** An Arrival event (weight 5, once per run) for a fleet with a free bunk. A small ship hails: it has a troublemaker aboard and pays the players to take them.
481. **Accept.** The chooser gets `TroublemakerPay` (0.25) event money units: a quarter of a standard mission's pay at the tier, split like a mission reward. The troublemaker joins as 1 crew member on a ship of the chooser with a free bunk. The ship leaves.
482. **Roll.** When the troublemaker joins, a roll from the page seed decides: with `TroublemakerThiefChance` (0.3) they are a thief. Else they stay as normal crew, and nothing more happens.
483. **Theft.** At the first station visit (the troublemaker's ship within `TroublemakerStationDistance` (300) of a trade station's hull), a thief takes `TroublemakerTheftFactor` (2) times the pay from the player who took them in, at most all of that player's money. The thief leaves the crew. A center text and a chat line name the thief, the amount and the station.
484. **Refusal and death.** A refusal costs nothing: the ship leaves. A troublemaker who dies or leaves before the station visit steals nothing, and the players keep the pay.
485. **Co-op, save and tests.** The accept runs in the choice input and the station check in the sim tick, with the state in the run state (`FriendlyState.Troublemakers`). Test: `friendly-troublemaker` (refusal, thief, honest, death before the station). The rolls in the test are fixed by the chance (1 or 0).

### The scientist

Code: `src/HotCargo/Friendly/Scientist.cs`. Pages: `scientist`, `scientist_partner`, `scientist_partner_yield`, `scientist_revenge` in `mod/events/events.rules`. Spawns: `ScientistPartner`, `ScientistRevenge`. Numbers: the `Friendly.Scientist*` and `Friendly.Partner*` tunables.

486. **Offer.** A Station event (weight 5, once per run, carried by the station, requirement 768) for a fleet with a free bunk: a scientist asks to join the crew.
487. **Join.** The scientist joins as 1 crew member on a ship of the chooser with a free bunk. Their research roll is due `ScientistMinutes` (10) game minutes later.
488. **Research.** When the roll is due and the scientist is still aboard, a roll from the page seed decides: with `ScientistResearchChance` (0.75) they research. The research unlocks one vanilla tech at no cost, picked at random from the `ScientistCheapestTechs` (5) cheapest techs that the players can research now (not unlocked, prerequisites met). A center text and a chat line name the tech.
489. **Defection.** Else, at the first AI ship with crew that comes within `ScientistDefectRange` (500) of their ship's hull (a station counts), the scientist takes up to `ScientistStealProcessors` (5) vanilla processors from their ship. They leave the crew and become crew of that ship. They walk out through an airlock and fly over, as any crew member with a new home ship does (requirement 1614 helps when that ship has no airlock). A center text and a chat line come once they have landed on the other ship.
490. **Death.** A scientist who dies aboard before the roll gets a chat line. `PartnerMinutes` (10) game minutes later, their partner comes. Limit: a scientist who leaves the sim in another way (a player fires them) counts as dead.
491. **Challenge.** The partner (`ScientistPartner`: one combat ship one tier above the system, strong for one player ship) spawns near the players and hails with the challenge: a duel of one player ship against theirs, with no other ships. The partner has its own allegiance (`Scientist.Allegiance`). It is hostile to the players only in the duel and the revenge, and never to anyone else. While it challenges or yields, its weapons are off.
492. **Duel.** On accept, the chooser's ship nearest to the partner is the champion. The partner attacks only the champion (a hunter override). When the champion is gone, the partner leaves. When the partner dies, the chain ends.
493. **Interference.** When another player ship, or a ship that a player hired, fires within `PartnerInterferenceRange` (400) of the partner's hull during the duel, the partner curses the players and jumps out. The revenge comes `PartnerRevengeMinutes` (15) game minutes later. Limit: it does not know what that ship fired at.
494. **Yield.** When the partner's health falls below `PartnerYieldHealth` (0.35), it yields, and the players stop being its enemy. It hails: it wants to join the crew.
495. **Revenge and decline.** A declined challenge brings the revenge too: the partner curses the players and jumps out. When the revenge is due, `ScientistRevenge` (three combat ships two tiers above the system, with the hunter AI) spawns near the players, hails in harsh words and attacks. The chain ends when the fleet is gone.
496. **The partner joins.** When the players accept the yield, the partner joins as 1 crew member. Their ship goes home. With `PartnerResearchChance` (0.8) they research `ScientistMinutes` later (requirement 488); else nothing happens. The partner never defects and has no partner of their own. "Go home" sends the partner away with no reward.
497. **Jumps.** A jump ends a challenge, a duel, a yield or a revenge attack. A partner or a revenge that is due still comes, in the system where the players are.
498. **Co-op and save.** The choices run in the choice inputs, and everything else in the sim tick and the AI tick. The state is run state (`FriendlyState.Scientists`, `FriendlyState.Partner`), and the rolls use the page seed. Text is UI only.
499. **Tests.** `friendly-scientist` (research, defection, a fair duel, the yield and the partner's research) and `friendly-scientist-revenge` (a hired ally breaks the duel; a declined challenge). The rolls in the tests are fixed by the chance (1 or 0).

### Wanted and hunted

470. When the players are wanted (vanilla: a faction in the system hunts them with police) and heat reached `WantedWarningMinStage` (default 1, the first stage), a warning shows once: a short message at the top of the screen and a chat line that names the factions and the ways out. Ways out: pay the bounty (hail one of their police ships; the vanilla surrender dialogue offers to pay it), leave the system (wanted belongs to the system), outlast the search timer, or a Wanted event (requirement 473). The warning shows again only after the players stop being both. Wanted alone stays vanilla.
471. The Wanted trigger: when the warning in requirement 470 shows, a Wanted event opens with chance `WantedEventChance` (default 0.7). It ignores the cooldown and the per-system cap. It does not open while another page waits.
472. A need can require wanted status (`Wanted = true`). An outcome can clear wanted (`ClearWanted`): every faction in the system stops wanting the players, as a paid bounty does.
473. Wanted events: A Clean Record (pay 0.4 + 0.01 × heat money units, with a sell-out risk that grows with heat; or pay one crate for a sure clear and heat −5) and Decoy Run (pay 0.5 money units, with a failure risk that grows with heat; or a Sensor Array ghost). Each opens once per run.
