## 35. Balance

BALANCE.md holds the economy model, the measurements and the reasons for the numbers. `docs/balance.html` shows them as charts. `tests/balance/` holds the measurement scripts (not part of `autotest-all.sh`); `tools/balance/model.py` turns their logs into the model.

300. **Scarce fuel.** FTL fuel (hyperium) is scarce, so a jump is a real decision. Every jump to another system costs fuel: a human ship pays the map jump fuel at the relay (requirement 650). The station fuel floor (requirement 304) keeps a run from getting stuck. Balance tunables (requirement 2411): the buy price is 150 (vanilla 50), a stack holds 10 (vanilla 20), stations stock 25 % to 40 % of their typed fuel tiles and 0 % to 2 % of their untyped tiles (vanilla 80 % to 90 % and 0 % to 10 %), and a jump costs twice the vanilla fuel (1 fuel per 100 mass for each 5 LY, and for each 12.5 km of a local jump). So a station holds about a fifth of the vanilla fuel. Hunters carry 60 fuel each (requirement 192), and hyperium asteroids keep the vanilla yield, so hunters and mining are the best sources.
301. **Blueprint price.** A blueprint costs its rules cost × the faction reputation factor × `Journey.TechCostScale` (default 2.5), rounded to 100. A patch on `CommTechsTab.GetTechCost` applies it, so the buy button and the buy input agree. All base blueprints together cost about 19 % of a run's income, and the overclock blueprints cost more than a run earns, so a player does not unlock all tech.
302. **Heat above the fame cap.** A mission reward or a kill above the vanilla fame cap of its tier gives no fame, but it adds the heat of its uncapped fame (requirement 25). Patches on `FameReward.GiveReward` and `CareerSimModeManager.OnShipJunked` add the heat for the part that the cap took. They run in the sim.
303. **Removed.** (Peak tier cap: it kept short visits to faction home systems from raising the tech value. The run map has no faction home systems, requirement 2552.)
304. **Station fuel floor.** Every station keeps at least `Balance.StationFuelPerMillion` (default 100) fuel per million credits of the ship value maximum of the next tier, and at least `StationFuelMinimum` (default 10): one relay jump of 10 LY for a fleet of the next tier at 70 % drive efficiency (10 fuel at tier 1, 30 at tier 7, 100 at tier 12). A station gets the floor when it first spawns, after the vanilla NPC fuel fraction (`OnShipSpawned`), and stations that are live at a new run or a system switch get it then. A trade ship never buys a station below the floor (`TradeRoutesManager.GetDesiredTradeDeltas`); players can buy all of it. The floor counts the fuel that a station holds: its stock for sale plus the fuel that its own crew move from one of its storages to another. Vanilla takes such fuel off the sale until the crew drop it (a planned pickup in `MetaShip.GetTotalTradeResources`), so the stock for sale can dip below the floor for some seconds. A station that goes into stasis keeps the fuel in its crew's hands: vanilla remembers only the fuel on storage tiles, so the mod adds the carried fuel to the remembered stock. Military stations get it too, but most have almost no storage. Limit: a station that refuses the players (requirement 657, from heat 90) does not sell its floor.
305. **Hunter tier floor.** With `Hunters.RouteTierFloor` on (default), every stage response and strike wave starts from the higher of the system tier and the peak tier of the run (requirement 19). So a jump back to a low system late in the run does not make the hunters weak. Informant waves (requirement 104) and mission waves keep the system tier.
306. **No dominant route.** The balance model (`tools/balance/model.py`) compares one sector in each zone (neutral, corporate ally space, shelter, corporate watch; requirements 641 to 643 and 664) by total value: mission money plus the tech value gain. It fails when a zone beats neutral by more than `ZONE_LEAD_LIMIT` (20 %, user decision 2026-11-27), with a line that names the zone and its lead. `scripts/check-balance.sh` runs it and also fails when the committed `tools/balance/model.json` or `docs/balance.html` differs from a new run. CI `build` runs that script. Design pillar 2209.
