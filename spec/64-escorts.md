## 64. Escorts

An escorted ship waits for the players before it leaves. Code: `src/HotCargo/Missions/EscortObjective.cs`, `src/HotCargo/Missions/EscortHail.cs`, `src/HotCargo/Missions/EscortMeeting.cs` (the meet step that both escort objectives run). The escort missions are Convoy Duty (requirement 36) and the chain stage missions `ChainEscort` (The Fence's Ledger, Navy Tender), with the objective `HotCargoEscort`, and the Mining Escort (requirements 1860 to 1867), with the objective `HotCargoMiningEscort`. Vanilla 0.30.5a has no escort objective (`Modes/Career/Missions/Objectives/`).

1850. **Wait for the answer.** After the accept, the escort does not fly its route. It starts only when a player answers its hail with "Ready" (requirement 1852). The ambush (requirement 36) and the arrival check run only after the answer. Escort missions have no time limit, so no clock runs while the escort waits. The objective line reads "Meet <escort>. It comes to you", after the first hail "Answer the hail of <escort> when you are ready", then "Escort <escort> to the jump point".
1851. **Come to the players.** While the escort waits and is live, its AI origin is the meeting spot: on the line from the nearest player ship (significant ships, lowest ship ID on a tie) to the escort, `EscortMeetGap` (300) from both hulls. It comes closer when the player ship's comms circle (vanilla `SensorManager.DetWorldCommsCircle`) would not reach it with `EscortCommsMargin` (50) to spare, so the player can always call it, but never into the player's hull. The spot is at most `EscortMeetRange` (3,000) from the escort's start, so the escort does not chase the players across the system. Past that range, the players come to the escort. An escort in stasis does not move, like every ship far from the players.
1852. **The hail.** When the hull of the nearest player ship is within `EscortHailGap` (800) of the escort's hull, the escort hails that ship like a vanilla AI hail (`CareerSimModeManager.AIHailPlayer`: the comms button flashes, a call widget shows). A chat line says "<escort> hails you: ready to leave?". The escort's comms page shows only "We are fueled and loaded. Ready to leave?", the choice "Ready. Lead the way." and the button "Not yet. Hold here.". "Ready" is a shared choice of the domain `escort` (requirement 600 and later): co-op players see hovers and votes, and the pick goes through the shared choice input. "Not yet" only closes the window. The page answers every call while the escort waits, also without a hail.
1853. **No answer.** The escort waits with no time limit. While a player hull stays within `EscortHailGap`, it hails again every `EscortRehailSeconds` (60 s). Reason: a timeout that starts the route brings back the playtest problem (the escort left while the players looked for it). The players can abandon the mission in the mission log.
1854. **Find the escort.** While the escort waits, the mission's map area is a ring of `EscortMarkerRadius` (600) around the escort, not the destination. The objective line focuses the camera on the escort, like before.
1855. **Start from the meeting spot.** On "Ready", the route starts where the escort is: the ambush share counts from there. When the straight line from there to the destination passes a sun center closer than the route's `SunClearance` (requirement 36), the escort first flies back to its start, where the checked route began, and then to the destination. A chat line says "<escort> is under way. Stay close.".

### Mining Escort

Code: `src/HotCargo/Missions/Mechanics/MiningEscortObjective.cs`. Rules: `mod/missions/mechanics/sysgen_mechanics.rules` (group `hc_mech_miningescort`), `mod/missions/mechanics/ai_miner.rules`. Numbers: `Mechanics.MiningEscort` tunables.

1860. **Offer.** Trade stations offer it in `Mechanics.MiningEscortChance` (50 %) of the systems (requirement 131). The field is the nearest asteroid field (POI tag `asteroid_field_poi`) to the station that has asteroids with ore within `FieldRadius` (3,000) of its center and no ship hostile to the players within `HostileRadius` (1,000) of it. Field pirates and platforms sit within 750 of the center; wanderers and bounty targets nearby do not block the offer. Vanilla puts pirates or defense platforms in two of three fields (`sysgen_asteroids.rules`), and a civilian miner avoids them. The ore is the most common ore of the field's asteroids, live or in stasis; a tie goes to the lower resource ID. A system without such a field has no offer. The objective spawns the miner when the offer is made, `SpawnDistanceMin` to `SpawnDistanceMax` (500 to 800) from the station center, as a civilian of the station's faction. The miner is the vanilla career starter Model-M (one mining laser, two storage bays), listed as the built-in ship `yarden_zamir.miner` with the tag `hc_miner`. Its AI `HotCargoMiner` has no modules, so it follows only the objective's orders.
1861. **Goal.** When the miner is first live, the goal is its free room for the ore, at most `OreGoal` (30). Ore that it already holds then does not count. 30 is about 2.5 to 3 minutes of mining for the miner alone: a sim run mined 25 hyperium in the first 2.3 game minutes.
1862. **Work.** The miner meets the players and waits for "Ready" (requirements 1850 to 1854). Then it flies to the field center with vanilla move orders. Within half of `FieldRadius` it starts mining. Every `StepSeconds` (0.5 s) it mines the nearest asteroid with its ore like an autonomous miner (requirement 1070: the work spot, `MineDistance`, `MineReach`, at most `SalvageJobs` salvage orders, with the ore as the collect type). Its mining laser fires on those orders, and its crew carries the ore into the hold. An asteroid in stasis gets a move order to its place.
1863. **Waves.** One wave comes when the counted ore first reaches each share of the goal in `WaveShares` (0.25 and 0.65), so two waves come, also when a big drop passes both shares at once. A wave has `ShipsBase` + `ShipsPerTier` × tier ships (1 + 0.15 × tier, scaled for co-op, requirement 334), at most `MaxLiveShips` (5) live, of tier `ShipTierBase` + `ShipTierPerTier` × tier (the sector tier − 1), from the built-in ships with `ShipTags` (combat). They jump in 1,800 to 2,400 from the miner on a random side, on one hostile allegiance, and fight around the miner (vanilla Normal AI).
1864. **Help.** Each step, loose ore of the miner's type within `CollectRadius` (1,500) of its hull gets the vanilla collect order (`ManualResourceTransfer`, as requirement 1068), so its crew fetches it. Ore that players drop there counts like mined ore. Limit: the Model-M has two crew, so a large drop takes a while to carry in. Revisit if playtests show that help feels slow.
1865. **Return and pay.** With the goal in the hold, the miner flies to `DockGap` (200) from the station's hull. When its hull is within `DockedGap` (500) of the station's hull, the objective completes, and the pay waits at the station (vanilla `ReceiveRewards`). If no asteroid with its ore is left in the system and no loose ore of its type is near, it also flies home, and the job pays in full.
1866. **HUD.** The objective line says "Meet <miner>" or "Answer the hail of <miner>", then "Escort <miner> to the asteroid field", then "<miner> mines <ore>: <count>/<goal>. Drop yours near it to help", then "Escort <miner> back to <station>". The map ring marks the miner while it waits, the field (`FieldRadius`) on the way and while it mines, then the station.
1867. **Failure and pay.** The mission fails when the miner dies. Pay (BALANCE.md, finding 10): 2× standard money like its peer, a station rescue, 3× fame (the waves pay no kill fame, like Claim Jumpers), 2× reputation, contract terms like a convoy (free, trail weight 0.5, rival group `ore_claims`, informant 5 %). It sits next to Claim Jumpers (requirement 137), which stays.
