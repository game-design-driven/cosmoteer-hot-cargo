## 61. Crew breach

In vanilla, a crew member in space can enter a ship only through one of its airlocks (`SpaceCrew`, `Airlock.GetNearestOnShip`). A claimed hulk with no airlock, or a ship that lost every airlock in a fight, leaves its crew floating outside for good. With the breach, the crew cut their own way in. Code: `src/HotCargo/Breach/`. Part rules: `mod/breach/breach.rules`. Numbers: the `Breach` tunables section. Requirements 1610 to 1617.

1610. **Wait.** A crew member in space waits when its home ship has no working airlock and its hull is within `Range` (100) of the crew member. A working airlock is an airlock part (a breach too) that is not destroyed and works: its switch is on and it is not scorched. After `WaitSeconds` (10) of game time, the crew member starts to cut. A crew member that boards, dies, gets another home ship or moves out of range stops waiting.
1611. **Cut.** The cut takes `CutSeconds` (5) and is free. The crew member waits next to the hull. On a player ship, a chat line says when the cut starts and when the breach is open. One cut runs per ship. The cut stops when its crew member boards, dies or gets another home ship, or when the ship gets a working airlock.
1612. **Site.** The breach goes to the site nearest to the cutting crew member, of the `MaxSites` (12) nearest. A site is a cell next to a walkable tile of the ship (a part with a crew speed above 0) that is empty or holds a part that is not walkable (armor). The breach part must fit there. A site where both sides allow a door comes first. With none, the nearest site where the part fits gets a forced door. Ties go by cell. When no site fits, the crew member waits again.
1613. **Breach part.** `yarden_zamir.breach` ("Breach"): the vanilla airlock with its art, 250 health, 4 steel, and no build menu entry. The breach goes into the ship's design and its physical parts at no cost. It replaces what is at the cell, and a door links it to the walkable tile. So repairs keep it, and an AI self-repair rebuilds it. A player can remove it in the ship editor.
1614. **Entry.** Every crew member paths through a breach as through any airlock, in and out. The first crew member cuts the way in; the others walk in through it.
1615. **Scope.** The breach works the same for claimed hulks, player ships and AI ships. A ship with a working airlock never gets a breach, and normal airlock use does not change.
1616. **Co-op and save.** The wait and the cut run in the sim tick and read and write the run state (`RunState.Breach`: the wait times and the cuts). So every co-op machine places the same breach at the same tick. The chat lines are UI only. A save and a load keep the timers and the breach. A jump to another system clears the waits and the cuts.
1617. **Tests.** `breach-hulk` (a claimed hulk with no airlock gets crewed through one breach, and the breach stays across a save and load) and `breach-player` (crew of a hulk with airlocks walk in, with no breach; a player ship that lost every airlock gets its crew back in). Unit tests: `BreachModelTests`.
