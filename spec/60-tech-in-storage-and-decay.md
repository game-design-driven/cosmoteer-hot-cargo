## 60. Tech Vault of 4, tech in normal storage, decay

Code: `src/HotCargo/Tech` (`TechDecay`, `TechDecayModel`, `TechModel.VaultsNeeded`, `Vaults`, `VaultCard`, `FitVaults`, `StartingVault`, `VaultDropPatch`). Part rules: `mod/tech/tech_vault.rules`. Numbers: the `Tech` tunables section and the part rules.

Four 1x1 vaults of 2 units filled a whole storage bay of a starter ship. Players also had no way to keep tech for a while without a vault.

### Vault

1540. **Capacity.** The Tech Vault stays one tile and holds 4 units (Stolen Tech `MaxStackSize` = 4). Its art shows the units as separate items, like the vanilla processors in a storage tile (requirement 442). The vault has no roof, so the items always show: a closed roof made a full vault look empty. The code reads the capacity from the vault grid, never from a constant. Health (3,000) and cost (8 steel) stay. Designs and saves with vaults stay valid: a saved vault of 2 units holds 4 now.
1541. **Vault count from data.** The vaults that a ship needs for the starting tech are `StartingTechUnits` / units per vault, rounded up (`TechModel.VaultsNeeded`; default 8 / 4 = 2). The design tool (requirement 1016), Fit Tech Vaults (requirement 1472), the ship pick warning (requirement 1552) and the tests use this one rule.
1542. Removed: the 2×2 placement of an earlier draft. The placement of requirement 441 stays.

### Tech in normal storage

1543. **Storage takes tech.** Normal storage (a flexible storage grid) on any ship takes tech. Crews fill vaults first: a storage with a consumer (the vault) has a higher vanilla sort priority than a flexible storage tile (`BaseResourceStorage.GetSortPriority` 262,144; `FlexResourceGrid.ResourceTile.GetSortPriority` at most 196,608), so crew carry tech from normal storage into a vault with room, and collected crates go to a vault first. The vault is no source for automatic transfers, so tech never leaves a vault by itself. `Vaults.Add` fills vaults, then normal storage; `Vaults.Remove` takes from normal storage first. The fleet count includes normal storage (requirement 449).
1544. Removed: the rule that kept tech out of normal storage on human ships (`FlexGridBlocksTechPatch`).
1545. **Vault card.** A selected vault shows its contents after the health on the vanilla parts card: "Tech 3/4". A selected normal storage part with tech shows its tech and the time until the first unit breaks down. GUI only.
1546. **Fill toggle.** The vault has the vanilla consumption toggle of a resource consumer (`ConsumptionToggleButtonOffset`, as on the chaingun magazine). It shows in the Resources view and turns the fill of that vault on and off; crew then take no tech into it. It goes through the vanilla resources input (`MPUpdateResourcesInput`), so co-op stays in sync. No supply toggle: the vault is no automatic source (requirement 1543), so it would change nothing.

### Decay

1547. **Decay.** Tech outside a vault breaks down in air and heat. A stack in normal storage of a human or derelict ship (a storage tile holds up to 4 units as one stack), or in crew hands, gains wear in game seconds. At `DecaySeconds` (default 360, 6 minutes) of wear, a stack in normal storage is destroyed. A carried stack whose time ran out breaks down when crew set it down outside a vault. A toast, a chat line and a run log line say so. Crates in space do not decay. `DecaySeconds` = 0 turns decay off.
1548. **Back in a vault.** `DecayInVault` says what a vault does to a stack with wear. `Pause` (default): the wear stays, and the decay goes on when the stack leaves the vault again. `Reset`: the stack recovers fully.
1549. **Which stack.** The game stores tech as counts, so `TechDecayModel` follows each stack by its place: a vault tile, a normal storage tile or a crew member. A stack keeps its wear while it stays. A crew member that picks up tech takes the wear of the tile that its transfer job names as source; units that stay keep the tile's wear. A tile that receives tech takes the wear of the crew member whose job named that tile; on a stack that was there, the higher wear wins. A stack that moves on one ship without crew takes the highest wear that left that ship in the same tick. Anything else is new tech without wear (a crate, an event, a purchase). The tile tooltip of normal storage says "Outside a vault: air and heat destroy it in m:ss"; a vault tile says "In a vault: safe", or "decay paused, m:ss left outside" with wear.
1550. **Visible.** While tech sits in normal storage: the heat panel shows "n TECH DECAYING in storage, first lost in m:ss" under the tech line, the ship's blip shows "n DECAYING" after "TECH n", and a toast says so when tech first leaves the vaults.
1551. **Co-op.** The decay runs in the sim tick from sim state only, in a stable order (ships by ID, then crew by ID). The run state saves the wear (`Tech.Decay`), and the integrity hash includes the stack count and the summed wear. Vault transfers are vanilla crew jobs and inputs. The co-op step `tech.decay` checks that both machines destroy the same tech at the same time.

### Start

1552. **No refusal.** A career may start with a ship whose vaults hold less than the starting tech. The ship pick (requirement 1470) offers "Fit Tech Vaults", "Start without" and "Cancel", with a warning in red: "NO TECH VAULT!" (or "TOO FEW VAULTS!") and the decay rule in one line. At run start the tech that finds no vault goes into the ship's normal storage, and a large toast in the world says "NO TECH VAULT!" (or "TOO FEW VAULTS!"), the tech in storage and its time. Tech that finds no room at all is lost, and a notice says so.
1553. **Tests.** Unit tests: `TechDecayModelTests` (vault count, decay, pause and reset, the crew hand-over, instant moves). Game test `tech-decay`: a start without vaults, vault first from normal storage, decay with save and load, destruction. `tech-move`: a full vault taken apart while the other vault is full, its waiting tech across save and load (requirement 1554). `journey-newgame-check`: a vanilla starter may start. `tech-fit-vaults`: Start without. Co-op steps in `coop-economy`: `tech.decay` (the fill toggle and decay on both machines), `tech.move` (waiting tech of a moved vault on both machines).
1554. **Taking a vault apart.** Crew that take apart a vault of a human ship (the last hit is salvage damage: a rebuild, or a move with the grab tool) put no tech at risk, and no crate drops. Its tech goes to the other vaults of the ship. The rest waits on the ship (`TechState.Waiting`, saved in the run state and in the co-op integrity hash) and goes into the first vault of the ship that has room, usually the rebuilt vault. Only when the ship has no vault blueprint left to build (the player removed the vault), the rest goes into normal storage, where it decays (requirement 1547), and only tech that finds no room there waits. Waiting tech counts as the ship's tech (`Vaults.ShipTech`): the fleet count, heat, the loss timer, the score and the ship tag include it. It does not decay: a move is a normal action. The heat panel shows "n wait for a vault" under the tech line. No toast and no chat line: the player needs no notice. A ship that becomes a wreck drops its waiting tech as crates, and a ship that is gone from its system loses it; both show a red toast and a chat line. A ship in jump limbo, in stasis or in another system keeps it. Telemetry names the cause `vault_deconstructed` (requirement 1587). The autotest `tech-move` and the co-op step `tech.move` check it.
