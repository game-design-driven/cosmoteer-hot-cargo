## 66. Counter kiting

Enemies stop feeding a player ship that kites them: a ship that outranges them and hits them while they cannot hit back. They close in hard, call for help, regroup out of reach or break off, by personality and build. Attackers of a kiter come from the side that pushes it into a nebula or into the other attackers. Scope: every hostile AI of requirement 360. Code: `Ai/CounterKiteStrategy.cs` (`HcCounterKite` strategy, `HcAnvil` tactic). Pure math: `Ai/CounterKiteModel.cs`. Numbers: the `Tactics` tunables.

1900. **Outranged.** A ship is outranged by a human enemy ship on its team radar when that ship reaches it (edge to edge within its reach: its longest weapon range, weapons that are off included; a weapon that reports no range limit reaches the ship's radar radius) and the ship does not reach that ship (edge to edge beyond its own longest active weapon range). A ship without an active weapon is never outranged: it flees (requirement 194).
1901. **Kite read.** A ship that stays outranged for `KiteReadSeconds` (20 s) and loses `KiteReadLoss` (3 %) of its health (as in requirement 47) in that time is kited. The read starts again whenever the ship is not outranged, and after each `KiteReadSeconds` without the loss.
1902. **Answer.** A kited ship answers, first match: a ship with agility `CloseInAgility` (0.5) or more and the Brawl or Pincer trait closes in (requirement 1903); a ship with the Call trait calls for help at once (requirements 196 to 198) and holds out of reach while it calls (requirement 1904); a ship with the Team trait regroups out of reach (requirement 1904); any other ship breaks off (requirement 1904). Without personalities (`PersonalitiesEnabled` off) only the agility decides: close in or break off. Without the call behavior (requirement 202) a caller breaks off.
1903. **Close in.** The ship attacks the kiter at `BrawlRangeFraction` (0.5) of its own range, above kite, cover and bait. When the gap (edge to edge) did not shrink by `CloseInGain` (20 %) of its start within `CloseInSeconds` (15 s), the ship breaks off instead.
1904. **Break off.** The ship holds `BreakOffMargin` (600) outside the kiter's reach (requirement 1900), edge to edge, on its side of the kiter, with the guarded move (requirement 1033). A team ship holds on the side of its allies within `RegroupSearchRange`, so the team gathers out of reach. Hunters and other AIs hold the same way: no time limit, so a ship never goes back to feeding the kiter.
1905. **End.** An answer ends when the kiter is gone (destroyed, a wreck, no longer an enemy or off the team radar), when the kiter comes within the ship's own reach, when an ally of the ship has the kiter within its own range, or for a team ship when `RegroupMinGroup` ships of its team are within `RegroupRadius`. Then the ship fights as before, and a new read starts.
1906. **Anvil.** Two or more allied ships that attack the same target, while one of them was kited by it within `AnvilMemorySeconds` (60 s), take sides of the target that push it. They look for a point inside a nebula of an `AnvilNebulas` type (electron: half speed and no jumps; cloudy: less sight) at `AnvilNebulaStrength` (0.5) or more, on 3 rings of 12 points up to `AnvilNebulaRange` (4,000) around the target, nearest ring first. With such a point every attacker takes the far side of the target from it, spread `AnvilSpreadDegrees` (45°) apart like the pincer (requirement 45). Without one, each attacker takes the far side of the target from the other attackers' center. The anvil is above every other tactic of the attack angle. Tell: the enemies swing around the kiter before they come in, and a kiter that backs off backs into the nebula or into the other enemies.
1907. **Tells, switches, determinism, tests.** No marker, label or log line names a read or an answer (requirement 368). `CounterKiteEnabled` switches it for hunters, `CounterKiteOnVanilla` for the vanilla combat AIs (requirement 202). The read window, the answer, the kiter, the close in start and the last read are module state: saved with the ship and part of the integrity hash. All decisions run in the AI tick and read sim state only. Unit tests `CounterKiteModelTests`: outranged, the reach of a weapon without a range limit, the read, the answer pick, the close in limit and the anvil angle. Autotest `ai-counterkite`: a kited pirate closes in, breaks off when the kiter keeps the gap, and holds out of the kiter's reach.
