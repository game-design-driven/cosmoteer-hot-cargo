## 69. Tech watch

The players see at once when their tech is not in a vault of a player ship, and where it is. Code: `src/HotCargo/Ux/TechWatch.cs`, the pure rules in `Ux/TechWatchModel.cs`, the derelict marker in `Tech/TechMarkers.cs`. Switch and numbers: `Ux.TechWatch`, `Ux.TechWatchAlarmSeconds`, `Ux.TechWatchCrateGroupRadius`. Playtest 2026-11-24 (co-op): "while rebuilding my ship I did not notice that the tech was not picked back up in my new ship." The new design had no vault, crew took the vaults apart, the tech went to normal storage and decayed, and the run was lost.

2120. **Why not a mission.** The lines are a HUD list, not a mission in the mission log. A mission is sim state: it needs saves, the integrity hash, the mission manager and the mission loops of other areas. The list reads synced sim state and changes none, so it needs none of that.
2121. **Places.** One line for each place of the players' tech outside a vault of a player ship, the most urgent first, then the most units:
    - Design: a human ship whose design has fewer vault blueprints than built vaults and too little vault room for its tech (requirement 2124).
    - Storage: tech in normal storage of a human ship, with the time until the first unit decays (requirement 1547).
    - Derelict: a derelict ship with tech (requirement 449).
    - Crates: tech crates in space, one line for each group within `TechWatchCrateGroupRadius` (300) of the crate with the lowest ID.
    - Hunter: a hunter that took crates (requirement 446): destroy it.
    - Friend: a friendly ship that holds tech (requirement 574): hail it to get the tech back.
    - Waiting: tech of a vault taken apart that waits for the rebuilt vault (requirement 1554).
    A holder in another system names that system. Tech in crew hands has no line: crew carry tech in every vault move, and the decay rule covers it (requirement 1547). Tech that the players gave away has no line, because it left the game: a hunter deal and a surrender (`Vaults.Remove`), a sale to the Buyer, a station or a fence (vanilla trade), a hand-over (`FleetHold.Take`). The station crew that carry a sale count as the players' tech in the fleet count (requirement 449), but they carry it, so no line shows.
2122. **Box and click.** The lines show in a box under the heat panel, in the left HUD column, with the header "N TECH OUTSIDE YOUR VAULTS" (N without the Design lines, whose tech is still in a vault; with only Design lines, "TECH WILL LEAVE YOUR VAULTS"). The box hides when no line is left. A click on a line shows its place with the Hot Cargo objective focus (requirement 1873): another system opens the galaxy map, a ship on radar takes the camera, else its place. A line goes when its tech is back in a vault, or when its tech is lost; each loss already has its own message (requirements 445, 446, 577, 1547, 1554).
2123. **Banner.** When tech of the Design, Storage, Derelict, Crates or Hunter lines stays out for `TechWatchAlarmSeconds` (3) real seconds without a break, the error sound plays, a red banner says "N TECH AT RISK OUTSIDE YOUR VAULTS", and a chat line names the first place. It shows again only after all of that tech was safe again, so a move between vaults stays quiet. Friend and Waiting lines raise no banner: the players chose them.
2124. **Design warning.** When a human ship's design has fewer vault blueprints than built vaults, and the vault blueprints hold less than the ship's tech (`Vaults.ShipTech`), the warning comes at once, before crew take a vault apart: the error sound, the banner "<ship>: NO VAULT FOR N TECH IN THE NEW DESIGN" and a chat line that says the tech goes to normal storage and decays, and to undo or add vaults. It covers every way to change a design (load a design, clear the blueprints, delete or grab a vault), because it reads the blueprints, not the tool. A move with the grab tool keeps the vault count, so it gives no warning: the tech waits for the new vault (requirement 1554). Limit: it warns after the design change, not in a dialog before it; vanilla builds in blueprint mode, so crew have not touched the vault yet, and the vanilla undo restores it. Revisit if players apply designs that build at once.
2125. **Derelict marker.** A derelict ship with tech gets an indicator with the Stolen Tech icon and "TECH n", on the map and as an off-screen arrow. Vanilla shows no indicator on a junk ship (`ShipIndicatorSource.ShouldShowA`), so the tag of requirement 444 did not show there. The sim tick adds it once per ship through the non-deterministic queue, like the crate marker; it changes no sim state. `Tech.TechMarkers` off hides it.
2126. **Jump.** When tech in crates or derelicts stays in the system, the jump dialog (requirement 1565) shows "N tech stays in this system (crates, derelicts)." and asks "Jump anyway?", also when every ship is ready. Hunters and friendly holders keep their tech in that system (requirements 446, 577), so they do not count.
2127. **Co-op.** Every machine builds the lines, the banners and the dialog line from the synced sim state, for the tech of every player. Nothing here sends an input or changes sim state.
2128. **Tests.** Unit tests `TechWatchModelTests`: the alarm kinds, the crate groups, the design rule, the alarm delay and its reset. Game test `ux-tech-watch`: a sale shows no line; a friendly holder shows a hail line that clicks to the ship and raises no banner; crates show a line that clicks to their place, the crate marker and the jump line; the playtest rebuild (a full vault leaves the design while the other vault is full) gives the design warning at once, then the storage line. Screenshots: `tests/screens/tech-watch.txt` (`docs/screenshots/techwatch-crates.jpg`, `techwatch-design.jpg`, `techwatch-storage.jpg`).
2129. **Switch.** `Ux.TechWatch` off hides the box, the banners and the jump line.
