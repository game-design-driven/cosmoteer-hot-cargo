## 9. Heat

23. Heat belongs to the current sector and starts at 0 in sector 1.
24. Each simulation tick, heat increases by heat multiplier × `HeatPerGameMinute` × the zone factor × elapsed game minutes. The heat multiplier includes the difficulty heat rate (requirement 681). The zone factor comes from the controlling faction of the system (requirements 641 and 643), else 1. While anyone in the crew owes money, it is × `DebtHeatFactor` too (requirement 1425). Default: 1.14 heat per minute. Open contracts add a trail on top (requirement 101).
25. When the players earn fame, heat increases by heat multiplier × `HeatPerStandardMission` × (fame earned ÷ standard mission fame of the sector tier). Default: 1.425 heat for each standard mission fame unit. The standard mission fame includes the same factors as the payout: the difficulty fame factor and `RewardScale`.
    The division keeps "heat per mission" stable across tiers. A typical mission pays about 3.4 units, because a vanilla mission pays the kill fame of its targets and its own fame reward (a bounty pays 2 to 2.5 units, a base attack 6 to 15). So a typical mission adds about 4.8 heat × multiplier. Fame that the vanilla fame cap takes adds heat too (requirement 302).
26. Heat multiplier = `HeatMultiplierByUnits[fleet tech count]`. Default table for 0 to 8 units: `[0.25, 0.4, 0.52, 0.64, 0.76, 0.88, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2]`. A count above the table uses the last entry.
27. Heat does not increase while the game is paused. Game speed changes the rate, as it does for all game time.
28. When the players enter another system, a share of the heat stays: a jump removes the cut of its kind (requirement 650). A switch to a system where own ships wait, without a jump, keeps `HeatCarryOverFraction` (default 0.25).
29. Heat stays in the range 0 to 100. During a network outage every heat gain is blocked and losses still apply (requirement 921).
1817. **Paid heat cuts.** Every comms choice that sells a heat cut for money shares one factor (user, playtest 2026-11-24: "each offered to reduce heat for money, and it's always worth it"). A choice counts when an outcome cuts heat and it costs credits: its own cost, or a submit with a picked option that pays money (`EventRunner.IsPaidHeatCut`, derived from the rules). Factor = 1 ÷ (1 + recent buys × `HeatBuyFalloff` (0.5)) × min(1, heat ÷ `HeatBuyFullHeat` (60)): each recent buy weakens the next, and a cold trail gains little. The heat cut is the outcome's cut × the factor; the price stays. Each paid cut adds 1 to the recent buys (run state `HeatBuys`), which halve every `HeatBuyHalfLifeMinutes` (20) of game time. Below `HeatBuyCloseBelow` (0.25) the paid cuts close: the choice shows greyed with "Nobody sells you a cleaner trail now", a hail line with `Need { HeatBuyOpen = true }` (Patrol Chatter) does not show, and the buy that closes them adds a chat line. The choice text shows the cut that works now (requirement 1816). Not included: the hunter pay-off heat drop (`HandOverHeatDrop`, requirement 593), which ends a hunter wave and which a bluff also gives. Tests: `HeatBuyModelTests`, `events-heat-buy`.

With defaults and 8 units held, the players enter a sector with about 25 heat (requirement 28). Each typical mission adds about 5.8 heat from fame and about 2.7 heat from its contract (flat heat and informants), and time adds about 1.37 heat per minute. So a sector reaches the top stage after about 6.4 missions in about 18.5 minutes, or after about 55 minutes with no missions. BALANCE.md has the full heat budget.
