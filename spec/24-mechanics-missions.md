## 24. Mechanics missions

Missions that use game mechanics, not only combat. MISSIONS.md describes how each one plays. Code: `src/HotCargo/Missions/Mechanics/`. Rules: `mod/missions/mechanics/`.

130. Nine mission types use game mechanics: Drone Siege, Prototype Retrieval, Sunside Ore, Salvage Rights, Repossession, Storm Survey, Claim Jumpers, Listening Post and Mining Escort (section 64). Their objectives are the rules types `HotCargoHoldOut`, `HotCargoPrototype`, `HotCargoSalvageTow`, `HotCargoCapture`, `HotCargoSurvey` and `HotCargoMiningEscort`, plus the vanilla `DeliverResources` (a goods hand-over, requirement 2384) and `TimeLimit`. Accepted missions are saved with the career and load with it.
131. Each mission type has one spawner group with a `hc_mech_<name>` tag. Before a system generates, the mod sets the group's spawn chance from `Mechanics.<Mission>Chance` (default 0.5 each: no type in more than half of the systems, BALANCE.md finding 10). Rewards use the vanilla tier tables. Each mission carries contract terms (`HotCargoContract`) for the economy area (requirement 100 onward).
132. **Drone Siege**: drones attack a faction listening post. The objective completes after `Siege.Seconds` (300 s) of guarded time. Guarded means that a human ship, of any kind, is within `GuardRadius` (1,500) of the post. A player-built platform counts. Waves come only while the post is guarded: the first after `FirstWaveSeconds`, then one every `WaveSeconds`. Ships per wave = `ShipsBase` + `ShipsPerTier` × tier, capped at `MaxLiveShips` live wave ships. In co-op the wave and the cap scale (requirement 334). Wave ships are built-in combat ships of tier `ShipTierBase` + `ShipTierPerTier` × tier, in 1 to `ShipTierMax` (3), on one hostile side of their own. They keep vanilla targeting (`Siege.TargetFocus` 0): the mission is meant for a drone or a platform to hold, and the armed post defends itself. The mission fails when the post dies. The offer text hints at building a separate gun platform (requirement 2291).
133. **Prototype Retrieval**: the Prototype Core part (`yarden_zamir.prototype_core`, 8,000 health) goes on the carrier's outer hull the first time the carrier is live after acceptance, at a spot that the crew reach when the hull has one. The core needs no crew, so the build tool does not mark it "No Door Access". The mission is recovered when the carrier surrenders to the players. It is also recovered when the ship that holds the core is a wreck or a player ship and a human ship is within `RecoverRange` (400) of the core. The core part then goes away, and the core goes into the hold (requirement 1108). The mission fails when the core part is destroyed or the ship that holds it is gone. A split moves the core to the piece that has it.
134. **Salvage Rights**: the mission completes when a crewless freighter arrives at the station (requirement 1877). The players tow it, push it, or claim it with crew and fly it. It fails when the hulk is gone, the station dies or 25 minutes pass.
135. **Repossession**: the capture step completes when the target ship surrenders to the players or becomes a player ship. It fails when the target is gone. A wreck that nobody claims yet is not a failure. Then the players deliver the ship to the bank at the station (requirement 2380).
136. **Storm Survey**: three markers sit inside clouds or storms (gas clouds, electron clouds or ion storms). A marker counts scan time while a human ship with a working sensor part is within 400 of it. The mission completes when every marker has `Mechanics.SurveyScanSeconds` (15 s). A system with fewer than two markers offers no survey.
137. **Claim Jumpers**: it works like requirement 132, with the `Guard` profile (240 s, raiders of the sector tier − 1), around a civilian mining barge in an asteroid field. The vanilla ship graveyard POI loses the field tag `asteroid_field_poi` (`mod/missions/mechanics/actions.rules`), so the barge never sits on a graveyard, which can lie past the relays (issue #364). The wave ships go for the barge: `Guard.TargetFocus` (20) uses the convoy raider rule of requirement 36 (`Missions/ConvoyTargetPatch.cs`), so a raider values the barge like a ship with 20 times its own firepower. Vanilla targeting (`AIAggroEnemiesModule`: value ÷ distance^1.5, the current target × `TargetSwitchThresholdFactor` 6) turns it on a guard only when the guard scores 6 times more, about 25 times closer at equal firepower: when the guard is in its path. Being shot does not count, for convoys either. So the guards must kill the raiders before they wreck the barge; losing the barge fails the mission. Sim runs at sector 1: one small player ship that fights (the Defensive preset), with or without a one-gun platform, kept the barge alive and killed every raider; a one-gun platform alone lost it at 201 s (tests `mechanics-guard-focus`, `mechanics-guard-hold`). The Mining Escort (section 64) is the other guard job.
138. **Sunside Ore**: Helion Ore (`yarden_zamir.helion_ore`) forms only on Helion asteroids. The asteroids sit in one seam 2,000 to 3,300 from a single sun, inside its heat zone. The players hand 30 to 45 units to the nearest station within 30 minutes, on the shared delivery page (requirement 2384); the pay follows the amount. Binary suns and sector types with the spawn flag `hc_no_sun_mining` get no seam.
139. A mechanics mission pays a multiple of a standard mission at its tier. MISSIONS.md lists the multiples. Wave ships that the C# code spawns pay no kill fame, so the mission reward holds all of the fame.
140. **Listening Post**: a scout of a faction listening post follows the nearest player ship. The players start the scan from the mission panel when the scout is within `Mechanics.ScanRange` (3,500) of a hostile base. Then it works like requirement 132, with the `Scan` profile (180 s, waves every 45 s from the base side). Before each wave the players can order up to `MaxIntensivePerWave` (2) intensive scans. Each scan gives the next wave `IntensiveExtraShips` more ships and `IntensiveExtraTier` more tiers, and adds `IntensivePayBonus` (0.25) to the money factor. Its waves keep vanilla targeting (`Scan.TargetFocus` 0); revisit if playtests show that the scout is never in danger.
141. **Leave early** (requirements 132, 137, 140): after the first whole wave interval, the players can leave from the mission panel. The objective completes at once. The money rewards become the earned share × the money factor, and the fame rewards become the earned share. The earned share is the whole `WaveSeconds` intervals guarded ÷ the intervals in `Seconds`. Orders go through the MP input `HotCargoHoldOutOrder`. The input checks the order again when it runs.
2290. **A hold-out without the player.** A drone or a platform of the players keeps the clock of Drone Siege and Claim Jumpers running alone. Whether it also holds the target depends on its guns. An autonomous ship with a working Computer is significant (requirement 1059), so it keeps the clock running. Every non-junk human ship is a stasis root (vanilla `CareerSimModeManager.GetShipStasisMode`), so the target and the waves stay live while the player flies elsewhere in the system. Drone Siege drones attack the nearest human ship (`Siege.TargetFocus` 0), so the guard draws their fire. Claim Jumpers raiders go for the barge (requirement 137), so there the guard must kill them first, and a one-gun platform is not enough. The guard needs rows to fight (requirement 1061), for example the Defensive preset. Test `mechanics-holdout-ai`: a platform of one Computer, one small laser and a targeted reactor next to the post, with the Defensive preset, keeps the Drone Siege clock running and fights the drones while the player's ship waits at the station. Its laser reaches 260, so it must stand next to the target. In a sim run this one-gun platform lost the post at 265 of 300 s, and the barge at 201 of 240 s; a real guard needs more guns.
2291. **The hint.** The offer texts of Drone Siege and Claim Jumpers say that any ship of the players near the target keeps the clock running: a gun platform for the siege, a drone or a gun platform for the barge. The Claim Jumpers text also says that the raiders go for the barge. While the clock is stopped, the objective line says "No ship or drone near: clock stopped" (short, so the HUD shows it whole).

2292. **Where a mission is.** A click on the objective line of a Hot Cargo mission shows where the mission is, in the order of the vanilla objectives. In another system, the galaxy map opens on that system. In the system, the camera goes to the target ship when it is on radar, else to the mission's area marker, else to the target's place. A target far away in stasis has a place too. The galaxy map marks the mission's system (`GetFirstMapNode`), and the system map shows its area (`GetAreaLocation`). This holds for Repossession (`HotCargoCapture`), the prototype, the tow, the storm survey, the hold-outs (Drone Siege, Claim Jumpers, Listening Post), Mining Escort, the convoy escort and the hand-over (its contact, or the item to recover, requirement 2381). Sunside Ore uses the vanilla delivery objective. Test `mechanics-focus`. Playtest 2026-11-23: the Repossession line had no click while the freighter was far away.

### Mayday: a live distress fight

A distress call that plays out as a real fight in the world, which the players can join and change. No offer and no dialog. Code: `src/HotCargo/Missions/MaydayObjective.cs`, `src/HotCargo/Missions/Mayday.cs`. Numbers: the `Missions.Mayday*` tunables.

2430. **How it starts.** On arrival in a system, a mayday is due there with `MaydayChance` (35 %), `MaydayDelaySeconds` (45 s) after the arrival. It needs the big event pacer (spec 905, kind `Mayday`: at most `MaxPerRun` per run, `MinGapSeconds` after the last one). It starts by itself: a chat line and a red banner say "Mayday! <ship> is under attack", and the mission "Mayday" enters the log, with no accept.
2431. **The fight.** A civilian trade ship of a faction that is not hostile to the players (a built-in ship with the tag `trade`, of the sector tier) and its attackers spawn `MaydayDistanceMin` to `MaydayDistanceMax` (2,000 to 2,400) from the nearest player ship, on a side clear of suns. So the fight is live and in sight from the start. The attackers are `MaydayRaiders` (2) built-in ships with the tag `combat` of the sector tier − 1, scaled for co-op (requirement 334), on one hostile allegiance. They want the victim like convoy raiders want the freighter (requirement 36, `EscortRaiderFocus`), also where no player radar reaches (requirement 1885). The victim runs toward the nearest player ship: its AI origin follows that ship.
2432. **Win, lose and pay.** The objective completes when every attacker is dead and the victim lives. The victim then thanks the players in the chat, and the pay waits at the nearest station of the victim's faction (vanilla `ReceiveRewards`, requirement 2252): `MaydayMoneyFactor` (2) × the standard money of the tier and `MaydayFameFactor` (2) × the standard fame, like a station rescue. It fails when the victim dies or when the players leave the system before it ends. A failure costs nothing more: the players chose not to help, or could not (spec 2203, losing is fine).
2433. **What the players see.** A red ring of `EscortMarkerRadius` (600) follows the victim (the escort alone ring, requirement 1886), on the map and in space. The objective line reads "Mayday: save <ship>. <n> attackers left, hull <p> %", and a click shows the victim (requirement 2292). The victim's hull share and the attacker count change as the fight goes.
2434. **Co-op and tests.** The start, the spawns and the end run in the sim tick on every machine, with the sim random stream. Test `missions-mayday`: the start, the spawns, the focus on the victim, a win with the pay at the station, and a loss. Co-op: `mp:coop-core` (the mayday starts and the attackers spawn the same on both machines).
