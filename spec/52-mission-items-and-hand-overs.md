## 52. Mission items and hand-overs

A mission that says you carry something gives you a real item. A mission that says you hand something over, or meet someone, has a real action for it. `docs/mission-fidelity.md` lists the gaps that remain.

1100. Mission items are resources. Normal storage holds them, one unit to a storage slot, and stations do not trade them. A destroyed storage bay drops the unit as a loose crate (requirement 2370). A killed crew member drops a unit that they carry. Items: Sealed Case (`yarden_zamir.sealed_case`, Courier Job), Flight Recorder (`yarden_zamir.flight_recorder`, The Black Box) and Prototype Core (`yarden_zamir.prototype_core_cargo`, Prototype Retrieval).
1101. A mission with a `HotCargoHandOver` objective that has an item and `GiveOnAccept` (the default) gives one unit when it is accepted, inside the game's accept input. The unit goes to the accepting player's first ship, by ship ID, that has room. When no ship of that player has room, the accept input refuses the mission: the accept button is greyed out with "No free storage for the <item>", and a click shows a message. A mission that leaves the log without its hand-over (failed or abandoned) takes one unit out of the players' ships, when the fleet held it.
1102. `HotCargoHandOver` is an exchange in a contact ship's comms. `Page` names a Hail event with `HailTarget = Contact`. While the objective runs (its turn in a sequence), the contact's comms show that page's line, and no other line. The line stays after each use until the objective ends. An outcome with `HandOver = true` completes the objective. With an item, the page's choices take it as a `Cost`, and the line needs a unit in any of its forms (requirement 1117). `ContactAreaTag` marks the contact's area on the map until a player ship has the contact on radar, like vanilla `GotoShip`. Hand-over lines show also when random events are turned off.
1103. A hand-over fails when the contact is destroyed. With an item, it also fails when the fleet held the item and no unit is left that the players hold (requirement 2382), loose in the system, or in the storage of a derelict (junk) ship, for example a player ship that lost control. The check waits while no player ship is in the system (during a jump). A message tells the players that the item is lost. A chain stage that fails this way gives the `lost` result (requirement 166).
1104. Courier Job hand-over page (`courier_handover` in `mod/events/events.rules`): "Hand over the sealed case" completes the job. "Hand over the opened case" makes the contact react (requirement 1118). "Not yet" keeps the case. The case opens in the hold, not on this page (requirement 1115).
1105. Extraction (The Quiet Defector, `ChainExtraction`): after the escorts die, the transport's comms show "Get Okafor out." (`okafor_pickup`). Bringing her aboard needs a free bunk and adds 1 crew. The transport's hold is real cargo (requirement 1120). The Debrief page then keeps her, or hands her to the navy or the corporation (she leaves the crew alive, requirement 1109).
1106. An event need can ask for free crew bunks (`Bunks`): the free crew capacity of the human ships in the system.
1107. An item with `GiveOnAccept = false` must be recovered first: the objective line reads "Recover the <item>" until the fleet holds it. With a source ship it reads "Recover the <item> from the <ship name>" (requirement 2381). `SourceShipTag` names the ship that holds it; the objective puts exactly one unit into that ship the first time it is live. When that ship is gone before the players hold the item and no unit is loose, the hand-over fails. The Black Box: the recorder is in a junk capsule beside the hunter wreck; salvage it, then hand the recorder to the station's broker (`blackbox_broker`). The Playback page follows.
1108. Prototype Retrieval: when the crew lift the core (requirement 133), the nearest human ship with room gets one Prototype Core. With no room in any ship, the lift waits, and the objective line says so. Then hand the core to the broker in the station's comms (`prototype_broker`): hand it over, or ask for more: 0.5 money units more and heat +10 (requirement 1121).
1109. An outcome can remove crew that leave alive (`CrewLeaves`): they go without the death path, so no death counts and no casualty. The result line says that they left the ship. The Debrief page uses it when Okafor goes to the navy or the corporation.
2380. A hand-over can deliver a ship (`ShipTag`, no `Page`): it completes on its own when the ship arrives at the contact (requirement 1877) and no player crew is aboard (crew whose home is the ship, or who stand on it). While crew are aboard, the objective line says "Take your crew off <ship> first". The hand-over removes the ship: the contact takes it. A surrendered ship of a running hand-over does not leave the system, and its crew fly it to the contact. The hand-over fails when the ship is gone. Repossession uses it: after the capture, the bank at the station takes the freighter, with no comms step.
1141. An outcome can drop its gains in space (`DropNear`, a ship tag). Its Tech and resource gains drift loose as crates beside the ship with that tag that is nearest to a ship of the player who chose (requirement 2240): live, a wreck (a wreck keeps its tags) or in stasis. With no such ship in the system, they drift beside that player ship. The result line says that they drift loose. The crew picks them up like any crate.
1142. Chain stages whose text calls the reward cargo use `DropNear`. Courier Run: the courier's crew pushes the tech crate out of the airlock (`hc_ch_cargo`). Okafor's Depot: a tech crate drifts out of the wreck. Take It Back: the 3 processors drift out of Rook's wreck (`hc_ch_enemy`). No stage puts cargo straight into the hold.
1143. Hold the Pass promises no convoy: its text says to keep a ship in the ring until the timer runs out (requirement 222, `PassHoldSeconds`).
1144. Sundive: the salvage puts one Flight Recorder (`Recorder` in the rules) into the storage of the ship that finished it. With no free storage, the salvage waits and the objective line says so. The escape completes when a human ship that holds the recorder is out of every heat zone. The job fails when no recorder is left in the players' ships or loose in the system; the check waits during a jump. When the mission leaves the log, the client takes the recorder (one unit). A hand-over that leaves the log without its hand-over takes its unit back the same way (requirement 1101).
2381. **Where the item to recover is.** While a hand-over step reads "Recover", it shows where the item is. The objective line names the source ship by its name at mission creation, from the ship data, for example "Recover the Flight Recorder from the Small Storage Pod". A click on the line shows a loose unit of the item first, else the source ship (requirement 2292). The galaxy map marks the source's system. The source ship, while it holds the item, and each loose unit get a vanilla object indicator: the blip of the zoomed-out map and the off-screen arrow, with the item's icon and name. Vanilla shows no indicator on a junk ship or a crate. The markers go when the fleet holds the item. GUI only: the markers never change the sim. Test `chains-recover-marker`. Playtest 2026-11-23: "we killed a ship and not sure where flight recorder".
2382. **What the players hold.** One rule counts a resource that the players hold: units in the storage of human ships, plus units that the players' crew carry, aboard, on another ship or in space, also crew with no home ship (`FleetHold`). Event needs, event costs, the hand-over line and the lost check (requirement 1103) all use it. A cost takes from the storage of any player's ship first (by player, then ship), then from the carrying crew. A carried unit is short-lived: an idle crew member puts it back in storage, else throws it out of an airlock; with neither, the crew member keeps carrying it, because career mode never deletes a storable resource (vanilla `SimModeManager.AllowDeleteCarriedResource`). A hand-over line shows also while another page waits (requirement 2383). Test `events-item-carried`; the co-op step `events.courier` hands over an item from the other player's ship. Playtest 2026-11-23: the broker showed no "Hand over the flight recorder." line while The Archive page waited.
2383. **A hand-over line over other pages.** One event page waits at a time, but a hand-over line never hides behind another page. Opening the line parks the waiting page (`EventsState.Parked`): it stays, hidden, and comes back as soon as the hand-over page closes, or on the next sim tick when the hand-over page closed another way. One page parks at a time: while a hand-over page is open with a page parked behind it, other hand-over lines wait, and their objective line names the open page and the ship whose comms show it. A page that waits in the contact's own comms shows there instead. Test `events-item-carried`. Playtest 2026-11-24: "make sure dialogs like those show even if there are other dialogs".
2384. **Goods deliveries.** Every delivery of a common resource is a comms hand-over at its contact, never a sale in the trade tab. Every vanilla `DeliverResources` objective becomes one (`Events/GoodsDelivery`): from a rules spawner (Salvage Race, Sunside Ore, Reactor Shielding, and any vanilla rules) and from the vanilla Resource Delivery offers that career mode builds in code. The job takes `MinAmount` to `MaxAmount` units in all (a rules `Amount` range; a fixed amount gives `MinAmount` = `MaxAmount`). The players hand them over in one or more trips, because a small hold carries less: each trip takes 1 up to the units still open, on a slider. The job completes on the trip that brings the total to `MinAmount`; earlier trips count, and the objective line says how many are delivered. All of them use one page, `deliver_goods` (its option has `Resource = "@handover"`, which takes the hand-over's resource and range). The pay is linear in the total: each money reward of the job × total / `MaxAmount`, rounded to 100 credits. The option and the choice show the pay of a completing trip, and "n of MinAmount delivered" for an earlier one. The objective line reads "Bring <amount> <resource> to <contact>: hail it", and the line shows while the fleet holds some of the resource (stored or carried, requirement 2382). A goods delivery has no lost check and takes nothing back: the resource is the players' own. A suggested collection area (`SuggestedCollectionAreaTag`) stays marked while the fleet holds none. The trade tab shows no delivery lines. A `DeliverResources` objective from an older save stays a trade delivery until it ends. Tests `events-goods` (vanilla Resource Delivery), `mechanics-survey-mining` (Sunside Ore: trips of 20 and 10, complete at 30 of 45).
