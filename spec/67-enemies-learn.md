## 67. Enemies learn tactics

Enemies notice how the players fight, report it when they call for help or flee, and the next ships of their group come prepared. A sensor array gives the players early notice. This is not the storyteller's Intel (`docs/storyteller.md`, step 2 stays parked). Scope: every hostile AI of requirement 360. Code: `Ai/AiLearn.cs`, the habit watch in `Ai/CounterKiteStrategy.cs`, `Ai/AiReportsState.cs`. Pure math: `Ai/LearnModel.cs`. Numbers: the `Tactics` tunables.

1920. **Habits.** Each hostile ship watches three habits of the human enemy ships, while one is on its team radar. Kite: the ship read a kite (requirement 1901). Part focus: every `HabitSampleSeconds` (2 s) it sums its part health by kind (weapons, thrusters, and core: the `VulnerableParts` categories) and adds what each kind lost since the last sample; repairs do not take a loss back. There is a focus when it lost `FocusMinLoss` (5 %) of its full part health in all and one kind holds `FocusShare` (50 %) of that loss or more. Cloud use: requirement 1921.
1921. **Cloud use.** A human enemy ship fights from a cloud when it is inside a nebula of any type at `AnvilNebulaStrength` (0.5) or more and the watching ship is within its reach (requirement 1900).
1922. **Report.** A ship reports what it saw when its call for help completes (requirement 198) and when it starts to flee (requirement 193). The habits join the report of its personality group (requirement 361: a faction ID, `pirates` or `hunters`): kite and cloud use stay once reported, and a new part focus replaces the old one. The report becomes new again (age 0). A ship that saw nothing reports nothing.
1923. **Fade.** Each system entry makes every report one jump older. A report fades at `ReportJumps` (2) jumps old.
1924. **Prepared.** Every ship of a group with a report is prepared against the reported habits only. Kite: it reads a kite after `PreparedKiteReadSeconds` (8 s) instead of `KiteReadSeconds` (requirement 1901), and it takes the anvil (requirement 1906) on any target that outranges it, without a kite read first. Part focus on weapons or thrusters: that part kind counts as protected in the side profile (requirement 1030), so the ship turns its strong side to the players; core parts are protected anyway. Cloud use: requirement 1926.
1925. **Sensor notice.** A report goes out in reach of a player sensor (the reach of requirement 1301): every player sees "{part}: {group} report how you fight: {habits}." The first time in a system that a prepared ship has a human ship on its radar, while a human ship has a working `CallInterceptParts` part, every player sees "{part}: the incoming {group} know how you fight: {habits}." once per group and system. Strings: `mod/tactics/strings.en.rules`. No other text names a report or a prepared ship (requirement 368).
1926. **Cloud hold.** A prepared ship (cloud use) does not follow a player ship into a nebula. While its target (its attack target, else the nearest human enemy on radar) is inside a nebula at `AnvilNebulaStrength` or more and the ship is not, it holds `CloudEdgeMargin` (300) outside the nebula edge, on the line from the target, with the guarded move (requirement 1033). It finds the edge in steps of `CloudEdgeStep` (200) along that line. Its weapons fire as usual.
1927. **Switch, determinism, tests.** `LearnEnabled` switches requirements 1920 to 1926. The habits and samples are module state of `HcCounterKite`, saved with the ship and part of its integrity hash; the reports (`RunState.AiReports`) are run state and part of the career integrity hash. All of it runs in the AI tick and the system entry, from sim state. Unit tests `LearnModelTests`: the focus pick, the report merge, the fade, the edge search. Autotest `ai-learn`: a kited pirate reports the kite, the next pirate of its group reads a kite after the prepared time, and the report fades after two jumps.
