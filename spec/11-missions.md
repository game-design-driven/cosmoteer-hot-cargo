## 11. Missions

New mission types appear in the normal mission lists, next to the vanilla ones.

36. **Escort**: a friendly AI ship flies to a destination in the system. The players must keep it alive until it arrives (requirement 1877: its hull within `Mechanics.DeliveryRadius` of the jump point). The pay waits at the station. The ship is a civilian freighter next to a station. It leaves only after the players answer its hail (requirements 1850 to 1855). The destination is a jump point `EscortRouteShare` (default 0.65) of the nearest jump gate's distance from the system center away, so the route scales with the system. The route stays inside the gate ring and 4,250 from sun centers (the heat zone plus 500, `RouteSunClearance` in `mod/contracts/system_scale.rules`, requirement 105), so a route that starts next to a station always fits; if no direction fits, the offer drops. When the freighter covers `EscortAmbushAt` (0.3) of the route, 2 to 3 hostile raiders (sector tier − 1, `missions/convoy_ambush.rules`) appear `EscortAmbushLead` (1,800) ahead of it on the route. Vanilla AI ignores unarmed civilian ships (`CareerSimModeManager.ModifyAIAttackValue` divides their value by 10,000), so raiders value the freighter like a ship with `EscortRaiderFocus` (20) times their own firepower. The freighter's AI (`HotCargoFreighter`, `mod/missions/ai_freighter.rules`) has no strategy modules: the escort objective gives its move orders, so it keeps its course and speed near enemies, and its turrets aim on their own. Reason: with the vanilla Normal AI, every enemy on its radar turned its hull to that enemy (`AIAggroEnemiesModule.TacticalRetreat`), and it crawled at 2 to 40 units per second (test `missions-escort-radar`). The freighter of the chain escorts (Quiet Freight, Navy Tender) carries the convoy freighter tag too. When the mission completes or fails, the jump point marker goes away. Leftover raiders stay as a normal hostile group. The freighter moves only while a player ship is near it, and alone it draws raiders (requirements 1881 to 1883). When the mission completes, heat changes by `EscortHeatChange` (default −8, about one typical mission and a half): a registered convoy hides the players' trail. The offer shows this as a reward line.
37. **Fleet assault**: an allied AI fleet and the players attack a hostile station together. Reward when the station is destroyed. The station is the vanilla pirate base shape. The fleet is 2 to 3 faction military ships of one faction; the mission belongs to that faction.
38. **Shady offer**: a mission offer with a reward of `ShadyRewardFactor` × the normal money reward (default 2.0). Fame stays normal. It uses the shape of a vanilla fugitive bounty and sits in the bounty list. Tells: no faction (so no reputation reward), the high pay, and odd wording. Shady deliveries are not implemented, because a delivery needs a station or trade ship as the target.
39. When the players arrive at a shady mission location, it is a trap with chance `ShadyTrapChanceBase` + `ShadyTrapChancePerHeat` × heat. Defaults: 0.2 + 0.006 per heat point, so 80 % at heat 100. Arrival means a human ship within `TriggerDistance` (rules, default 2,500) of the target ship, or the target is destroyed. The roll uses the heat at that moment.
40. A trap spawns an ambush of 2 ships of the sector tier + 1 near the player ship nearest to the mission location (`mod/missions/shady_ambush.rules`). The ships are local-faction ships with a unique hostile player index and the hunter AI (`HotCargoHunter`). When the trap springs, a message and the wanted alert tell the players that it was a setup. A trap that the players survive pays `ShadyTrapSurvivalReward` × the normal money and fame reward (default 0.5). Survive means that all ambushers are destroyed. If the players leave the system first, the trap ends without that reward.
41. All new missions earn fame, and so they add heat through requirement 25.
42. New mission types use vanilla objectives where possible: `ProtectShips`, `DefeatShips`, `Sequential`, `AnyOrder`, `ReceiveRewards`. The game has no objective for "an AI ship reached a location" and no random objective, so the mod adds the rules types `HotCargoEscort` and `HotCargoShadyTrap` (objectives) and `HotCargoScaled` (a reward that multiplies a vanilla Money or Fame reward by a mission tunable). Allied ships are a sibling spawner group with faction allegiance; a child of a hostile station would inherit its allegiance.

Note: accepted Hot Cargo missions store the custom objectives in the save. A save with an accepted Hot Cargo mission does not load without the mod.
2250. A mission that leaves the log (completed, failed or abandoned) while the players are in another system cannot remove its target area markers at once: they lie in a system that is not loaded. The run remembers them by system (`EndedMarkers` in the run state), and the next arrival in that system removes them, unless a mission in progress or an offer owns them by then. An objective's markers lie in its first map node.
2251. **Visit missions** stay vanilla (`CareerGameModeManager` system visit missions): a galaxy offer for a system next to the current one, gated by fame (`FamePrereqPerLowTier`). It ends in two steps: the arrival in the system (`GotoNodeObjective`), then the receive payment button in the comms of any station of that system that gives missions (`ReceiveRewardsObjective` with the system as contact, `SimGlobalPendingRewards`). Until the payment the mission stays in the log. Test `missions-visit`: the accept input, the jump, the arrival step and the payment, because the path crosses the Hot Cargo accept patch and jump hook (playtest 2026-11-14: "Visit Titraness" stayed open because the players never called a station there).
2252. **Pay at a station.** Every mission of the mod that pays a reward (money, fame or reputation) pays it at a station, like vanilla missions: its objectives end with vanilla `ReceiveRewards`. A mission that the players get at a station pays there; a mission that spawns at the players (the fight events Rivals, Siege and Event Bounty, the chain fight stages Courier Hit, Station Raid, Collectors and Depot) pays at any station of its system. A chain stage gives its result when the pay is collected (requirement 166). Missions whose rewards are only contract terms (`HotCargoContract`: Off the Books, the Buyer sale) are page trades and pay on their page. Vanilla Ambush keeps its vanilla pay on completion. Test `missions-pay` checks every loaded mission spawner of the mod.
