## 68. Item art

Each Hot Cargo item (a resource with an ID `yarden_zamir.*`) has its own art, so it reads at a glance and differs from the other items and from vanilla resources. No item shows a tinted vanilla resource. Code: `tools/sprites/make_items.py` (the designs) and `tools/sprites/item_sheet.py` (a contact sheet next to vanilla resources). Workflow: `docs/part-art.md`, Items.

2000. **Own art.** The rules file of each item references its sprites from `sprites/<item>/sprites.rules` in its own folder: `Icon` (64 px), `Overlay` (128 px), `NuggetSprite` with its normal map, and one `StackSprites` entry for each unit up to `MaxStackSize`. `make_items.py` writes the PNG files and that `sprites.rules`. The script is the source, as for the part art (requirement 1057): no committed image is a copy or an edit of a game image.
2001. **Vanilla look.** The sizes and the look come from the vanilla resources (`./Data/resources/`): a 28 px object in a 64 px tile, a dark rim, light from the top left, a soft black shadow on all sides, and a normal map with green up. Stacks fill the slots of the vanilla processor stacks: the four quarters, then the middle. A glowing part adds a halo of its colour, like uranium. The overlay is white line art with a soft glow.
2002. **Designs.** Flight Recorder: an orange box with black rubber end caps, two white bands, a handle and a red lamp. Sealed Case: an aluminium courier case with a handle, latches, a red seal band and a lock with a red light. Opened Case: the same case open flat, the lid lining above and empty foam below, with the cut-out of its contents and a torn seal. Prototype Core: a blue glowing core held by four clamps in a dark frame with hazard corners. Rescue Beacon: a white puck with an orange ring, a whip antenna and a green lamp. Tracer Beacon: a small black hex puck with a wire antenna and a red light. Access Codes: a purple data card with gold contacts, a stripe and lines of code. Stolen Tech: a crate with steel corners and a mint tech glyph. Helion Ore: a dark rock split by glowing orange seams.
2003. **Lights.** Lights move through `AnimationFiles` with `AnimationStartTime = Random`, so the items in one hold do not blink together. Flight Recorder and Rescue Beacon: a short blink each second or so. Tracer Beacon: a fast double blink. Prototype Core: a slow pulse. Equal frames share one file.
2004. **Helion Ore.** Helion Ore has its own art too: the tinted vanilla gold looked like copper ore, and its overlay showed "Au". Its stacks use three rock shapes, as vanilla ore stacks show different pieces.
2005. **Parts of the items.** The Tech Vault and the Prototype Core part have own art too, drawn by `tools/sprites/make_sprites.py` in `sprites/tech_vault/` and `sprites/prototype_core/` next to their rules (requirement 1057 rules). Tech Vault: an armoured bed with a pad under each of the 4 units, a heavy frame with a locking bolt in each wall and mint status lights in the corners; no roof (requirement 1540). Its editor icon shows the vault full of tech crates. Prototype Core: the blue core of the cargo item in a containment ring, held by four clamps, with hazard pads in the corners, on the vanilla deck inside the vanilla battery walls; the core pulses. Both have damage levels and blueprints. `tests/screens/item-art.txt` shows the items and both parts in the game.
