## 75. Number breakdowns

A key number shows where it comes from on hover: each step with its size, the total, and what would change it (pillar 2211). Code: `Core/BreakdownModel.cs`, `Ux/BreakdownTextModel.cs`, `Ux/BreakdownText.cs`, `Ux/BreakdownAutotest.cs`.

2580. **One computation.** A number with a breakdown comes from a `Breakdown`: an ordered list of steps (base, plus, times, clamp, round). The total is the fold of the steps, and only a step changes it. The model function of the number returns the breakdown, and the game uses its total: the sim, a roll and the tooltip read the same steps, so they cannot drift apart. A step has a text key and an optional argument, not text. A step can name a hint (a player action) and the value of the step after it.
2581. **Tooltip.** The tooltip shows a title, one line for each step that changes the number by at least the last shown digit, a subtotal before an addend that follows factors, the total, then each hint with the change of the total that it gives. Round steps do not show. A change shows green when it is good for the players and red when it is bad. Factors show as percent by default (×1.20 is "+20%"); `Ux.BreakdownFactors` (`Percent` or `Multiplier`) switches all tooltips to "x1.20". In co-op the host's tunables apply to every player (limit: the setting is not per player).
2582. **Heat rate.** The heat line of the heat panel: the base rate (`HeatPerGameMinute`), the tech table factor (hint: sell 1 tech), the difficulty heat rate, the zone factor of the system (hint: leave corporate ally space or corporate watch), the debt factor (hint: pay your debts), then the contract trail (hint: finish or drop the contracts). Areas add their factors through `Run.Hooks.TimeHeatTerms`. During a network outage the rate is 0 (requirement 921).
2583. **Tech value.** The tech line of the heat panel: the base share of the peak tier ship value, the game time part, the notoriety part (each in credits, requirement 19), then the difficulty tech factor (requirement 683). Both rounds to 100 are steps.
2584. **Chances on the demand page.** The demand surrender button (requirement 567), the bluff button (requirement 565) and the pay-off button (requirement 594: each selected row, then the crowd factor, then the cap) show their chance steps in the button tooltip. A greyed button shows its reason first. The roll uses the same total.
2585. **Tests.** Unit tests `BreakdownModelTests`: the fold, the hints, each number's total against the formula of its requirement, the view's total text. Autotest `ux-breakdowns`: each tooltip opens on the real widget through the game's tooltip box, fails when the player cannot see the widget (inactive, off the screen or outside its scroll box), and its total line must show the number that the sim adds or the roll uses. Screenshots: `tests/screens/breakdowns.txt`.
