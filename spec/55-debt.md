## 55. Debt

A faction station lends money. The debt grows with interest, collectors come to ask for it, and a debt that runs away turns the lender hostile. Code: `src/HotCargo/Debt`. Strings: `mod/debt`. Numbers: the `Debt` tunables section. Pure math: `Debt/DebtModel.cs`.

1400. **Money unit.** Debt amounts use the event money unit (requirement 73) at the sector tier when the amount is set. "One mission's worth" is 1 unit.
1401. **Debts.** A debt belongs to one player and one faction, the lender. It has the principal (the amount lent) and the balance (what the player owes now). Each player can owe several factions, one debt each, and in co-op each player has their own debts. A loan, a refinance or a tab (requirement 1435) is the chooser's debt. The run state keeps every debt, the same on every co-op machine. Decision (user, playtest 2026-11-15): each player has their own debt; before, the crew shared one debt for each lender.
1402. **Loan offer.** A trade station of a faction offers a loan with chance `LoanStationChance` (0.5), seeded by the station ID, so the offer holds for the run. It needs a running run, no debt to that faction, and a station that is not the Buyer. The station's debt page (requirement 1416) shows the loan: the amount (`LoanUnits`, 5 units), the interest, the collector times and the 2× and 3× rules.
1403. **Take the loan.** "Take the loan" pays the amount to the chooser and opens the debt: principal = balance = the amount. A chat line and a run log line name the lender and the amount.
1404. **Interest.** Every `InterestSeconds` (600) game seconds after the loan, the balance × (1 + `InterestRate` (0.05)), rounded up to whole credits. Interest runs in every system and stops while the game is paused.
1405. **Repay.** Any operating station of the lender (trade or military) and every collector of the lender (requirement 1407) show the balance and a pay row: a slider from `PayStepShare` (0.1) of the balance up to the whole balance, in steps of that share, no higher than the chooser's money. The chooser pays. The last step always pays the exact balance. A balance of 0 closes the debt. A chat line says what was paid and what is left; a paid-off debt gets a run log line.
1406. **Stage.** Ratio = balance ÷ principal. The stage is calm below `FirmRatio` (2), firm from `FirmRatio`, and hostile from `HostileRatio` (3). A payment lowers the ratio, and so the stage. Each stage rise gets a chat line and a run log line.
1407. **Collectors.** While the players owe a faction and that faction does not want them, a collector comes every `CollectorSeconds` (2,400) game seconds, counted from the loan. A collector is 1 combat ship of the lender (its military player index, the sector tier or the nearest lower tier). It jumps in `CollectorDistance` (700, inside comms range like an event carrier) from one player ship (requirement 1323). It flies to the nearest ship of the player who owes the debt until it is in comms range, then hails it like an event carrier. Its comms page shows only the debt part: other areas' offers (hire, hold tech) do not show. It waits `CollectorWaitSeconds` (150) game seconds from its arrival, then jumps out. A payment or "Not now" sends it away at once. A paid-off debt sends its collectors away. A collector does not come in a system with no human ship, and the timer then waits.
1408. **Collector tone.** A collector's page says the balance, the ratio, the next interest and the stage rules. Calm: a polite reminder. Firm: a firm warning that names the 3× limit. Hostile: the last warning, "pay it below 3× or we hunt you".
1409. **Hostile.** When the ratio reaches `HostileRatio`, the lender wants the players: the vanilla wanted state of the current system (`WantedManager`). Its ships are enemies and its police come (vanilla `WantedPoliceCount`). The debt stays and interest goes on, but no collector comes while the lender wants the players. A center text, a chat line and a run log line say so.
1410. **Asked again.** Vanilla ends the wanted state when the lender's ships lose sight of the players for the search time, after a surrender, and in another system (each system has its own wanted state). Then the next collector comes within `ReturnSeconds` (180) game seconds. A collector at the hostile stage that leaves with the ratio still at `HostileRatio` or more makes the lender want the players again, after "Not now" or after its wait. That collector does not jump out: it stays and fights. A player who attacks a collector gets the vanilla wanted state anyway.
1411. **Score.** The score (requirement 7) is money + units still held × the Buyer unit price − `ScoreDebtFactor` (2) × the sum of the balances. So a loan that is not paid back costs its amount once. After a win, interest and collectors stop, and the debt keeps its value.
1412. **Display.** The heat panel shows one line for each debt of the local player: the balance, the lender, the ratio, the time to the next interest and the time to the next collector. The firm stage shows in the warning color. The hostile stage shows in red, with "HUNTED" while the lender wants the players.
1413. **Co-op.** Every debt choice is a shared choice (domain `debt`, section 32), so co-op players see hovers and can vote. The page serial is the ship ID of the station or the collector. At a station, a choice acts on the chooser's own debts; at a collector or a helper, it acts on that ship's debt, and the chooser pays. An option packs the kind and the amount in hundreds of credits. The sim checks the choice again when it applies (the page is open, the chooser has the money, the debt exists) and rejects it otherwise. The run state saves the debts, their timers and the collectors. The integrity hash includes the sum of the balances and the collector count.
1414. **Determinism.** Timers count `RunState.GameSeconds` in the sim tick. The collector ship and its spot come from the sim random generator. The loan offer roll is a `FastRand` seeded by the station ID.
1415. **Tests.** Unit tests `DebtModelTests`: interest, stages, the pay steps, the option code and the score. Autotest `debt-core`: a loan, interest, a payment at a station, a collector at 1×, 2× and 3×, the wanted state, a collector after the wanted state ends, and the score. The station's normal page has one debt line, no debt choices and its other lines; the debt page has only the debt part; a collector's page has nothing else (requirement 1416). Co-op step `debt.loan` in `coop-economy`: the client takes a loan, the host pays part of it, both machines show the same balance, no desync. Screenshots (`tests/screens/debt.txt`): `docs/screenshots/debt-loan.jpg`, `debt-collector-1x.jpg`, `debt-collector-2x.jpg`, `debt-collector-3x.jpg`, `debt-panel.jpg`. Autotest `debt-overdraft`: a build pre-purchase that takes money below 0 opens a debt with the system's faction up to the credit limit, a pre-purchase at the limit buys nothing, an unchecked spend borrows only to the limit, the hail after 60 s at the limit, no hail below it, no hail after the debt is paid, and the tooltip lines. Co-op step `debt.overdraft` in `coop-economy`: the client's overdraft is the client's own debt, the same on both machines. Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/debt-money-tip.jpg` (`tests/screens/debt-money.txt`).
1416. **Debt page.** A station's normal comms page shows one line for the debt: "Ask about a loan." when it offers a loan, else "Talk about your debt." when it takes payments or buys a debt (requirements 1402, 1405, 1421). The line opens the station's debt page. The debt page shows only the debt part: the loan offer, the refinance offers, the debt state, the pay row and "Back". Other areas' lines (for example "Sell a crate off the books.") and the vanilla dialogue stay on the normal page. A collector's page is always a debt page, without other areas' offers and without the vanilla dialogue. Which page a player sees is local UI state, never saved or sent; the choices still go through the shared choice input (requirement 1413). Limit: a player who listens in on a hail (section 47) sees the speaker's normal page.

### Money below 0

1417. **Overdraft.** In each sim tick, a human player whose money is below 0 borrows the shortfall, up to the credit limit: the money goes back toward 0, and the amount goes on that player's debt with the lender (requirement 1401), or opens a new one. The credit limit is `OverdraftLimitTier1` (20,000) × the vanilla standard mission pay of the system's tier ÷ that of tier 1 (tier 1 to 8: 20,000, 27,000, 33,000, 40,000, 50,000, 64,000, 82,000, 106,000 credits). It counts the player's balance with that lender. A build order pre-purchases its resources at once with no money check (vanilla `MPUpdateShipInput`, construction mode `CrewResources`, `ResourceManager.PrePurchaseBuyableResources`); in a run it buys only what the player can pay, money plus the credit left, so the rest of the build waits. Other spends that do not check money (a delivery, a surrender payment) borrow up to the limit, and past it the money stays below 0 until the player earns or pays back; vanilla buy checks then stop new spending. Paying back, or less debt with that lender, gives room again. Vanilla has no credit line of its own: money below 0 is only the result of these unchecked spends. The lender is the controlling faction of the current system (`MapNode.ControllingFaction`, as for zones and paid help, requirements 641 and 1430); without one, the faction of the operating station nearest to that player's ships; without one, the corporation's rival (requirement 640). Then interest, stages and collectors apply as for any debt. A chat line names the amount and the lender. Telemetry event `debt.overdraft`: player, lender, amount, balance, and whether the debt is new. Before, money could stay below 0 with no debt (playtest 2026-11-15: 18 to 36 minutes; a build order at heat 78 went from 5,290 to −16,185). The start money is not below 0 with the co-op starters (10,600 for each player with the fast economy, 600 with the slow one), so no debt opens at the run start.
1418. **The lender gets in touch.** A debt that an overdraft opens comes due for a hail `OverdraftHailSeconds` (60) game seconds later. If that player still owes it, its balance has reached the credit limit (requirement 1417), and the lender does not want the players, a collector of that debt comes at once (requirement 1407) and hails that player's nearest ship. Its page starts with a line in the lender's voice: the string `OverdraftHail_<faction ID>`, else `OverdraftHail`. One hail for each debt: the due time clears when it comes, whether the collector comes or not. Telemetry event `debt.hail`: player, lender, balance, cause `overdraft`. Decision: the hail was for a balance above 20,000; with the overdraft capped at the limit (20,000 in tier 1), the hail is for a debt at the limit, so it still comes in tier 1 and scales with the tier.
1419. **Money tooltip.** The tooltip of the money display shows, under the vanilla text: the local player's credit left with the lender that would cover money below 0 here (requirement 1417); then each debt with its lender, balance and interest; the total; and what the stages mean in plain words (requirement 1406). Every debt amount in the game (the tooltip, debt pages, the hail, chat and run log lines, a pay-off reward line) shows in the vanilla "bad" red (`<bad>`), the red the mod uses for debt and danger lines, with a red money icon.

### Debt missions

1420. **Pay-off rewards.** A mission whose client is a lender (`Mission.Faction`) pays the debt instead of money with chance `MissionPayoffChance` (0.35), seeded by the mission ID. So the roll is the same on every machine and holds for the mission. While the players owe that faction, the money line of the mission reads "Debt −N to the <lender>" in place of the money, in the offer, the mission log and the payout. N is the vanilla money (`MoneyReward.GetScaledMoney`). Each player's vanilla share goes off that player's debt to the client (requirement 1401); money above the balance, and the share of a player without that debt, pays as vanilla money. A chat line names the amount. Without the debt, the mission pays money.
1421. **Refinance.** A trade station offers to buy a debt with chance `RefinanceStationChance` (0.3), seeded by the station ID. Its faction must not be the lender, not the corporation's ally (requirement 640), and the players must owe it nothing. The page shows the balance it pays, the new interest (`InterestRate` × `RefinanceRateFactor` (0.6)) and the reputation that the old lender loses (`RefinanceReputationLoss`, 200). "Refinance" closes the old debt and opens a debt to the station's faction: principal = balance = the old balance, the lower interest, timers from now. The old lender's reputation falls by that amount, and its collectors leave. A wanted state of the old lender stays (vanilla). A chat line and a run log line say so.
1422. **Collect a debt.** While the players owe a faction, a trade station of that faction offers "Collect a Debt" once in each system (the run state saves the systems). A debtor ship (a combat ship of the sector tier − 1, its own hostile side) waits `CollectDistance` (3,000 to 7,000) from the station. Defeat it, by kill or by its surrender. The reward is `CollectRewardFactor` (1.5) × a standard mission's money and a standard mission's fame. The money always pays the debt (requirement 1420), whatever the roll. It is a contract (requirement 100) with no fee and 2 flat heat.
1423. **Not picked.** "Work it off" (escort the lender's convoy) repeats Convoy Duty (requirement 36) with a different reward, and requirement 1420 already gives that. "Carry a hot crate" needs the hand-over item flow (section 45) for one more heat-for-money trade. Revisit both when a debt needs more ways out.
1424. **Tests.** Unit tests `DebtModelTests` (the pay-off split). Autotest `debt-missions`: a pay-off reward line and payout, a refinance with the reputation loss and the new rate, and a collect job that pays the debt. Co-op step `debt.loan` in `coop-economy`: a loan and a payment change the one shared debt the same on both machines.

### Paid help

1430. **Help comes.** Once in each system, when at least `HelpMinEnemies` (2) enemy ships of the players are within `HelpFightRadius` (3,000) of a human ship, the controlling faction of the system rolls `HelpChance` (0.3) to send help. The roll is a `FastRand` seeded by the system, so a fight without help leaves the sim random stream as it was. The faction must not be the corporation's ally (requirement 640). The players' reputation with it must be at least `HelpMinReputation` (0), it must not want the players, and the players must not owe it at the hostile stage. The run state saves the systems that rolled.
1431. **Helpers.** `HelpShips` (2) combat ships of that faction (its military player index, the sector tier or the nearest lower tier) jump in `HelpDistance` (1,200) from one player ship (requirement 1323). Their AI fights the players' enemies. A center text and a chat line say who came and that they will want pay.
1432. **Damage share.** Every damage hit on an enemy of the players counts while the helpers are in the system: the hit's damage (`DamageEffectRules.Damage`, with buffs) goes to the helpers when a helper fired it, and to the players when a human ship fired it. Hits by other ships do not count. Share = helper damage ÷ (helper damage + player damage). Limit: a hit counts its damage value, not the health that it took (shields, armor and damage pools are not measured); the same rule holds for both sides.
1433. **After the fight.** The fight ends when no enemy of the players is within `HelpFightRadius` of a human ship for `HelpCalmSeconds` (20) game seconds. With a share of `HelpMinShare` (0.1) or more, the first live helper flies to the nearest human ship and hails it (like a collector, requirement 1407). Its page is a debt page (requirement 1416), without other areas' offers (hire, hold tech). With a lower share, the helpers jump out and a chat line says they did not help. If the players leave the system first, the helpers stay behind and ask nothing.
1434. **Price.** Price = share × `HelpPricePerShare` (4) event money units (requirement 73), rounded like event money. The page shows both shares ("You did 60 % of the damage; we did 40 %.") and the price.
1435. **Choices.** All three are clear deals, and each button names its result:
     - "Pay": the chooser pays the price, the helpers jump out.
     - "Put it on my tab": the price goes on the debt with that faction (requirement 1401): it adds to the principal and the balance of a debt that exists, or opens a new debt. The helpers jump out.
     - "We didn't ask for help": the button names the rule and the share, "(works when they did less than 25 %; they did 40 %)". With a share below `ClaimMaxShare` (0.25), the helpers leave and the players pay nothing. Otherwise the faction's reputation falls by `ClaimReputationLoss` (150), and the helpers leave unpaid.
     Decision: the claim depends on the share that the players saw in the fight, not on a hidden chance (design rule, section 2). The user asked for a chance; the PR text says why the share rule is better.
1436. **No answer.** The helper waits `HelpWaitSeconds` (120) game seconds after its arrival at the players. Without an answer, the price goes on the tab (requirement 1435) of the player whose ship is nearest the helper, and the helpers jump out.
1437. **Co-op.** The choices are shared choices of the `debt` domain; the page serial is the hailing helper's ship ID. The damage counts, the help group and its timers are run state, and the damage hook runs in the sim on every machine.
1438. **Tests.** Unit tests `DebtModelTests`: the share, the price and the claim rule. Autotest `debt-help`: help comes in a fight, real hits count for both sides, the hail after the fight, and each choice (pay, tab, claim that works, claim that fails), and that the asking helper's page is the debt page alone. Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/debt-help.jpg`. In test scripts the help trigger is off unless a test sends `debt_help_on`: every test starts in the same seeded system, and an extra fight side would change the exact counts of other areas' tests.
1439. **Reserved.**
