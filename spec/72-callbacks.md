## 72. Callbacks

Consequences that come back later. A mechanic records a memory of what the players did and to which ship. In a later sector, that ship comes back and hails with a callback page that names the past act. Code: `src/HotCargo/Events/Memories.cs`, pure rules `Events/MemoryModel.cs`. Pages: the `cb_*` events in `mod/events/events.rules`; their ship: the `Returning` spawn in `mod/events/spawns.rules`. Catalog: EVENTS.md, "Callbacks".

2500. **Memory.** A memory is one past act of the players: its kind (`MemoryKind`), the plain name of the other ship, that ship's faction (empty for no faction), the system name and the sector index. The run state saves the memories in the order they happen (`EventsState.Memories`). The co-op integrity hash counts them and the recalled ones. A memory is general: a later feature adds a kind (a clue, for example) without a new store.
2501. **What is remembered.** An accepted surrender: through a demand, a hunter's yield or the vanilla offer (the diplomacy event `Deals.Spared`). A faction ship leaves `Spared`, a ship with no faction leaves `SparedPirate`, and a hunter leaves nothing (`MemoryModel.SparedKind`). The faction comes from the ship's team, because a surrender truce negates its player index. A hunter's pay-off that it takes leaves `PaidOff`, and a bluff that it believes leaves `Bluffed` (`Deals.Decided` with the offer accepted). A refused pay-off or a failed bluff leaves nothing. A won mayday leaves `Rescued` with the saved ship (spec 2432). Every record runs in sim logic (a shared choice input or the sim tick).
2502. **Text.** A memory shows only on its callback page: the tokens `{mem_ship}` and `{mem_system}` in the page text, the choice texts and the outcome texts. The rules check fails an event that uses them without `Recalls`.
2503. **Callback event.** An event with `Trigger = Callback` and `Recalls = <kind>` (the two go together, and a callback needs a `Carrier`). It is eligible only while a memory of its kind exists that no callback recalled and that is at least `CallbackMinSectors` (1) sectors old. When it opens, it recalls the oldest such memory: the memory is used up (each memory comes back once), the page keeps a copy, and the carrier ship takes the name of the ship in the memory. Limit: the carrier is a new ship from its spawn, not the old design.
2504. **Trigger.** The Arrival point of a new sector (spec 69) first tries a callback, while at least one memory is not recalled: a roll of `CallbackChance` (0.5), then the cooldown and the per-system cap (spec 70), then a weighted pick of the eligible callbacks (spec 71). When no callback opens, the normal Arrival roll runs. An Arrival point comes once per sector, so a sector gets at most one callback. With no memory to recall, the try makes no roll.
2505. **Rare.** Callbacks are the big event pacer kind `Callback` (spec 905, 906): at most `MaxPerRun` (2) per run, `MinGapSeconds` (900) apart, and the gap after any big event. A memory that waits stays for a later sector. Reason (pillars 2209, 2210): a callback is a rare echo of a choice, never a reason that makes one choice the best.
2506. **The spared.** `cb_spared` ("An Old Debt", recalls `Spared`): the ship thanks the players. Fly with us: the ship joins the chooser's fleet (effect `join`, spec 1361). Share what you can spare: 0.5 money units. Tell the patrols: heat −10. Go in peace: nothing. Each answer except the join sends the ship away (effect `leave`).
2507. **The spared pirate.** Two callbacks recall `SparedPirate` with the same weight, so a spared pirate comes back as a friend or a foe at even odds. `cb_pirate_debt` ("Pirate's Honor"): a pod of processors and steel drifts beside the ship, or the players refuse it. `cb_pirate_grudge` ("Unfinished Business"): pay 0.6 money units, or fight three pirates (`TollGuards`) while the named ship leaves.
2508. **The hunters.** `cb_route_sold` ("Sold Out", recalls `PaidOff`): pay 0.5 money units, fight a corporate patrol, or let them talk (heat +15). `cb_bluff_grudge` ("That Wasn't All", recalls `Bluffed`): hand over one crate (one crate stays), or fight a corporate patrol. Every answer costs something: a pay-off and a bluff have a price that can come back.
2509. **The rescued.** `cb_rescued` ("Thank-You Run", recalls `Rescued`): the saved freighter gives steel and coils, a quiet word (heat −10), or a good story (fame +0.3).
2510. **Co-op and tests.** The records, the trigger and the choices run in sim logic on every machine; the choices are shared choices (spec 771). Tests: autotest `events-callbacks` (a pirate's surrender, the memory across a save and a load, no callback in the same sector, the callback in the next sector under the ship's name, the pacer gap, and every callback page), memory checks in `diplomacy-hunters` (pay-off and bluff) and `missions-mayday`, co-op step `events.callback` (`tests/autotest-mp/coop-friendly.txt`), unit tests `MemoryModelTests`.
