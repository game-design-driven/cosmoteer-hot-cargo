## 62. The storyteller: adaptation

The hunters adapt to how the run goes. A crew that loses ships or crew meets lighter hunter waves for a while. A crew that plays without losses meets heavier waves, more often. This copies the adaptation of RimWorld's storyteller in a narrow band. Heat stays the clock: the heat stages start the waves as before (section 10). The background, the comparison with RimWorld and the parked step 2 (a plan of beats, Intel) are in `docs/storyteller.md`. Code: `src/HotCargo/Storyteller`. Numbers: the `Storyteller` tunables section. Pure math: `Storyteller/StorytellerModel.cs`.

1720. **Switch.** `Enabled` (default on) turns the adaptation on or off. Off, or while the Pursuit is off (requirement 900): the pressure is 1, and the run plays as before. Autotests start with it off, like the Pursuit (requirement 943); a test turns it on with `story_set Enabled true`.
1721. **Adapt minutes.** The run state saves adapt minutes, from `AdaptMin` (−30) to `AdaptMax` (60). A new run starts at 0. They count for the whole run; a jump keeps them.
1722. **Time.** Each game minute of the run adds `AdaptPerMinute` (1) adapt minutes. In sector 1 they do not rise above 0, so the hunters of the first sector are never heavier than the base (RimWorld's first 30 days).
1723. **Losses.** A human player ship that is lost takes `AdaptLossShip` (20) adapt minutes. Each crew member of a human ship who is killed takes `AdaptLossCrew` (1). A ship is lost when it leaves the human ships of the system other than by a jump: it became a wreck or was destroyed. A crew member is killed when the vanilla count of killed crew of a human ship rises (`ShipCrewManager.KilledCrew`); a rehire does not give minutes back.
1724. **Curve.** The pressure is a straight line between three points: −30 adapt minutes → `PressureMin` (0.85), 0 → 1.0, 60 → `PressureMax` (1.25). Adapt minutes outside −30 to 60 do not exist (requirement 1721). Choice: the steps are the RimWorld shape (0.4 at −30, 0.8 at 0, 1.47 at the top) shrunk to the band, with 1.0 at the start, so a new run plays as before.
1725. **Impact.** The pressure moves toward 1 by the combat difficulty's impact: pressure = 1 + impact × (curve − 1). `AdaptImpact` lists one impact for each vanilla combat difficulty level (no_death 1, easy 1, normal 0.9, hard 0.7, very_hard 0.5); a level without an entry counts as 1. So mercy after a loss is smaller on hard levels, as in RimWorld. A level that is not a vanilla combat level is a tunables error.
1726. **Stage size.** The ships of each hunter stage response × the pressure, next to the ramp and the fleet factor (requirements 901, 902). The product of the fleet factor and the pressure stays within `CombinedMin` (0.75) to `CombinedMax` (1.4), so a rich crew without losses does not get both maxima. The co-op scale, the difficulty factors and `MaxLiveHunters` apply after it, as now (requirements 31, 334, 682).
1727. **Strike interval.** The repeat Strike wave interval ÷ the pressure, next to the Pursuit's interval factor (requirement 901), through `Run.Hooks.StrikeWaveIntervalFactor`.
1728. **No mid-fight change.** The pressure applies when a stage response or a Strike wave is sized. A wave that is already in the system keeps its size. So a loss lowers the next wave, never the one that is on its way.
1729. **Determinism.** The storyteller reads sim state and run state only, in the sim tick, and uses no random roll. The career integrity hash includes the adapt minutes.
1730. **Telemetry.** `story.profile` (category Story): once per system visit, at the system entry, with `adapt` (adapt minutes), `pressure`, `impact`, `sector`, and the losses of the last visit: `ships_lost` and `crew_killed`. Long stays: the time use of each system stay is measured by telemetry (section 36, #193); the storyteller does not change for long stays yet (`docs/storyteller.md`, option L0).
1731. **Tests.** Unit tests `StorytellerModelTests`: the curve points and the band, the sector 1 cap, the loss steps and the clamp at both ends, the impact, the combined clamp. Autotest `story-profile`: with the storyteller on, game time raises the adapt minutes, and in sector 1 they stop at 0; in a later sector the pressure rises above 1; a lost player ship lowers the adapt minutes by `AdaptLossShip` at once; a stage response sizes its ships with the pressure (the log line); with the storyteller off the pressure is 1.
1732. **All fights.** With `AllFightsEnabled` (default on), the pressure also multiplies the co-op factor (requirement 331) of every hostile spawn that is not a hunter stage response or a Strike wave: rules groups and fleets (requirement 332, for example pirate groups, base defenders, fleet bounties and ambushes), mechanics waves and their caps (requirement 334), pirate thief groups and the wanted police cap (requirement 335). Hunter stage responses and Strike waves take the pressure once, in requirement 1726. A single ship (a bounty target, a mission carrier, a capture target) stays single, and faction military does not scale (requirement 332). The pressure applies when the spawn is sized: a system generates at its first visit with the pressure of that moment (requirement 1728).
1733. **Never none.** The co-op factor × the pressure can be below 1 (solo, pressure below 1). Then a spawn or a cap of 1 or more ships stays 1 or more, and the spacing of a group stays as it is (requirement 332).
1734. **Tests.** Unit tests `ScalingModelTests`: a factor below 1 and the floor of 1. Autotest `story-profile`: at the top pressure the fight factor is above 1; with `AllFightsEnabled` off, or the storyteller off, it is 1.
