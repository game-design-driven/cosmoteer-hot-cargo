## 27. System archetypes

WORLDS.md holds the design. The archetypes give each system an identity, a hazard and a matching mission. They add no jumps.

220. A system of the run map gets a sector type by weight: four Hot Cargo archetypes (Trade Hub `hc_trade_hub`, Mining Belt `hc_mining_belt`, Solar Zone `hc_solar_zone`, Deep Space `hc_deep_space`) and the vanilla types at lower weights. The weights are the tunables `Worlds.SystemTypeWeights` (requirement 2414); `mod/worlds/archetypes.rules` (the vanilla `RandomSectorTypes` spawner, used by `mod/journey/galaxy.rules`) holds the types and their tier limits. A change applies to new runs only. The starting system stays basic. Deep Space needs tier 3 or more. Each archetype is the basic sector plus spawn flags (`mod/worlds/sectors.rules`), so every spawner that an area adds to the basic sector runs in it too.
221. **Trade Hub**: a hub world (1.5 × an inner planet, no other inner planets) next to a trade station, and a current: a straight band of the `hc_current` cloud, 6,000 long and 2,400 wide (rules type `HotCargoCurrent`; fixed sizes, not times the system scale), along the flow direction of the system. The flow direction is one of 8 directions, picked from the map node seed, so it is the same after a load and on every client. Each sim tick, the nebula effect `HotCargoCurrentPush` pushes every live ship in the band along the flow with an acceleration of `CurrentAccel` × cloud strength × (1 − speed along the flow ÷ `CurrentFlowSpeed`), clamped to 0 to 1. It also pushes across the flow, toward the nearer side of the band (outward), by the same rule with `CurrentAccel` × `CurrentSideShare` and `CurrentFlowSpeed` × `CurrentSideShare`. The band axis runs through the mean of the current triangles. It never brakes a ship. Loose items in the cloud (vanilla nuggets: resources, loot and tech crates) get the same push, and their vanilla drag does not apply while they are in the cloud, so they move like ships without engines. A ship or item that only drifts moves at the angle atan(`CurrentSideShare`) from the flow and leaves the band; with the default 0.25 (about 14°), drift from the band center leaves at the far end, and drift from more than about 450 off the axis leaves at its side. Stations, asteroids and megaroids do not drift. Arrows drift over the band in the drift direction (`CurrentArrows`); the vanilla nebula tooltip and HUD line name the current.
222. **Mining Belt**: a dense belt of large rocks and some megaroids, 900 wide, in the middle of the gap between the trade station ring and the gate ring (requirement 105; about 8,200 from the center), with a rock count that follows the orbit length (about 1,140 to 1,280), instead of the vanilla belt. 3 or 4 passes (ring markers of radius 800) cut through it with no rocks inside. Pass rings and rocks keep clear of station and gate hulls. **Hold the Pass** (`HotCargoHoldPass`): first the players defeat the raiders who camp in one pass (vanilla `DefeatShips`). Then the hold clock runs while a human ship is within the ring. After the first entry, the mission fails when the ring stays empty for `PassLostSeconds`. Every `PassWaveSeconds` of holding, a wave (`PassRaiders` in `mod/worlds/spawns.rules`) comes from `PassWaveDistance` away. The objective completes after `PassHoldSeconds`. Away from the system, the clock waits.
223. **Solar Zone**: a single vanilla sun with its vanilla heat zone (the kill radius is 1,500 and the zone edge is 3,750 for the default sun). Depth = 0 at the zone edge and 1 at the kill radius; the vanilla heat grows with depth. A HUD line shows the deepest own ship in a heat zone and its depth, red from `HeatWarnDepth`, in every system with a sun. **Sundive** (`HotCargoSundive`): a wreck site at depth 0.15 to 0.25. A human ship within 300 of the site salvages; after `SundiveSalvageSeconds` of salvage time that ship gets the recorder in its storage (requirement 1144), and the objective completes when a human ship that holds it is out of every heat zone. It fails when the recorder is lost. The heat also burns Tech Vaults like any part; a vault loses its tech only when it is destroyed (requirement 65). The Sunside Ore seams (requirement 138) sit on the sun too. Other missions can target this archetype with the spawn flag `hc_solar_zone`.
224. **Deep Space**: the vanilla dense nebula sector plus 2 or 3 more ion storms. A pirate lair (the vanilla pirate base shape, hidden until discovered) sits in a calm eye (radius 1,800) of its own ion storm, 8,000 to 20,000 times `SystemScale` from the center, with the vanilla military station gaps times the scale (requirement 105). **Raid the Lair**: `HotCargoFindLair` shows a search circle of radius 4,500 that holds the lair, with the lair 20 % to 60 % of the radius from its center. It completes when a human ship comes within `LairDiscoverDistance` of the lair, or when the lair is gone. Then the players destroy the lair (vanilla `DefeatShips`).
225. On the first arrival in a system, after `ArrivalLineDelaySeconds`, a center text (the sector type, with the hazard below it) and a chat line name the sector type and its hazard (`ArrivalLine`). The galaxy map tooltip shows the same hazard line under the vanilla sector type line. The hazard lines are strings `HotCargoWorlds/Hazard_<sector type ID>`; a type without one shows no line.
226. Accepted archetype missions save their objective state with the career: the hold time, the empty time and the waves of a pass hold, the salvage time and the held recorder of a sundive, the lair and the search circle of a raid. A save with an accepted archetype mission does not load without the mod.
227. The current push, the objectives and the waves change the sim only from the sim tick (the nebula effect and `EventHub.Tick`). Waves use a random source seeded from the mission. Before a run is active (the first system of a new career), sim code uses the default `Worlds` tunables, not the local file.
228. Contract terms (`HotCargoContract`, requirement 100 and later): Hold the Pass is free, flat heat 6, trail weight 1, informant chance 10 %. Sundive costs 0.3 standard mission pay, flat heat 2, trail weight 0.5, informant chance 5 %. Raid the Lair is free, flat heat 4, trail weight 1, informant chance 20 %. Pay: Hold the Pass 2 × a standard mission's money and fame, Sundive 3 × money and 1.5 × fame, Raid the Lair 2.5 × money and 2 × fame.
229. Archetype objects fit the smaller Hot Cargo systems (requirement 105): the belt lies between the trade station ring (up to 7,000 from the center) and the FTL gates (9,375 to 11,250). The hub world keeps 2,500 from the gates. The autotest `worlds-fit` checks that real system generation places each archetype mission and keeps the archetype objects clear of stations and gates.

### Close the Pass: Hold the Pass in reverse

The players block a pass while a raider convoy tries to break through it. Code: `src/HotCargo/Worlds/ClosePassObjective.cs`. Rules: `mod/worlds/sysgen_worlds.rules` (group `hc_group_closepass`). Numbers: the `Worlds.ClosePass*` tunables.

2440. **Offer.** The nearest trade station of a Mining Belt system offers it in `ClosePassChance` (60 %) of those systems, at one of the passes (requirement 222). Text: "A raider supply convoy will run the belt through this pass. Close it: no freighter gets through." The offer group draws from its own random stream (requirement 1897), so it moves no other roll of the system. Pay like Hold the Pass: 2× standard money and fame, 3× reputation, the same contract terms (requirement 228). The pay waits at the station (requirement 2252).
2441. **How it starts.** After the accept, the pass ring shows, and a clock of `ClosePassDeadlineSeconds` (300 s) runs: "Close the pass: be in the ring before the convoy comes (4:12)". The convoy comes when a player ship's hull is inside the ring, or when the clock runs out. Away from the pass system the clock waits, like Hold the Pass. A chat line and a red banner say "Raider convoy inbound!".
2442. **The convoy.** `ClosePassFreighters` (2) built-in trade ships and `ClosePassEscorts` (2) built-in combat ships of the sector tier − 1 (scaled for co-op, requirement 334), on one hostile allegiance, jump in `ClosePassRunLength` (3,000) outside the pass, on the far side from the system center. The freighters fly through the pass center to the exit point, `ClosePassRunLength` inside the pass, and keep flying when hit: they have the module-less AI of the Ore Tender (requirement 1868), so only the objective's move orders fly them, with no flee and no surrender (with the vanilla Normal AI they turned and fled from nearby fights in a test). The escorts guard the lead freighter and fight the players (vanilla Normal AI). A red ring marks the exit point.
2443. **Win and lose.** The objective completes when every freighter is dead. It fails when a freighter arrives at the exit point (the shared arrival rule, requirement 1877), or when the clock runs out while no player ship is within the live range of the pass (2,500): the convoy runs through unseen. The players can also abandon it. A failure costs only the pay (spec 2203, losing is fine). Destroyed freighters drop their cargo like any ship.
2444. **What the players see.** The objective line reads the clock before the convoy comes, then "Stop the convoy: <n> freighters left". The pass ring and the exit ring show on the map and in space, and a click on the line shows the pass, then the lead freighter (requirement 2292).
2445. **Co-op and tests.** The clock, the convoy spawn and the end run in the sim tick on every machine, with the sim random stream. Test `worlds-close-pass`: the start by a ship in the ring, the run through the pass, a win, the pay at the station, a loss at the exit, and a loss by the clock. Co-op: `mp:coop-core` (step `worlds.close-pass`, with the mission built in its system, because the session does not jump to a Mining Belt).
