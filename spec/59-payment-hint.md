## 59. Payment hint

A mission that pays at "any station in a system" ends only when the players press the receive payment button in the comms of a station there. The players see that step when they arrive. Code: `src/HotCargo/Ux/PaymentHint.cs`, the pure rule in `Ux/PaymentHintModel.cs`. Switch: `Ux.PaymentHint`. Playtest 2026-11-14: "Visit Titraness" never paid, because the players arrived and never called a station there (the mission works: requirement 2251).

2400. **Payment hint.** A payment is due here when its vanilla objective (`ReceiveRewardsObjective` with a system as contact) is in progress, it is the active step of its mission (vanilla `Objective.GetObjectives` with `activeOnly`: a sequence reports only its current step, so the arrival comes first), and its system is the current one. While it is due, its objective line in the Nearby Missions list and the mission log reads "Call a station here to collect payment" in place of the vanilla "Receive payment from any station in the <system> System". When it first becomes due, one chat line and one banner say "<mission>: call a station here to collect payment.", once per mission, with no sound. A chain stage keeps its colored "[Chain]" mark in the chat line; the banner shows the title as plain text, without tags (playtest 2026-11-17: the banner showed raw color tags). GUI only: no sim state. Limits: the told set is not saved, so after a load an open payment hints once more; vanilla formats the line when it builds it (an arrival, an objective change), not in every frame. Tests: unit tests `PaymentHintModelTests` (due only after the arrival and in that system, once per mission).
